# Decision 033: Fox, Ghast, Panda, and Sea Turtle module unification and live gameplay integration

Accepted: 2026-11-14.

## Context
Four mob/ecology mechanics remained split across duplicate rule modules in `src/world/` with unused imports and discarded calls in `src/main.zig`:
1. **Fox & Sweet Berries**: `src/world/taiga_fox.zig` (21 tests: `FoxState`, pounce arc, snow-stuck timer, nocturnal sleep/wake, mouth food priority swap, Totem revival, Chorus Fruit warp, sword mouth damage) and `src/world/fox_sweet_berry.zig` (27 tests: `Fox`, `BerryFoxVariant`, `harvestBerryBush`, `pounceVelocity`, `berryBushDamage`, breeding). In `src/main.zig`, F-key `.sweet_berry_bush` interaction unconditionally yielded 2 berries without stage tracking, and `active_fox` ticked with fixed `false` daylight/shade flags.
2. **Ghast Fireball Deflection**: `src/world/ghast_deflection.zig` (16 tests: `deflectGhastFireball`, `resolveFireballImpactOnGhast`, `canGhastFireballDestroyBlock`, `canGhastSeeTarget`) and `src/world/ghast_fireball_tennis.zig` (38 tests: `Ghast`, `GhastAttack`, `GhastFireball`, `Loot`, `rollGunpowder`, `rollGhastTear`, `canExplosionBreakBlock`). In `src/main.zig`, `.hostile_fireball` projectiles could not be deflected by melee swings or player projectiles.
3. **Panda Genetics**: `src/world/panda_genetics.zig` (11 tests: `PandaGene`, `SneezeResult`, `resolveExpressedPandaPersonality`, `pandaMaxHealth`, `rollBabyPandaSneeze`, `canBreedPandasWithBamboo`, `isWorriedShakingHidingFace`, `canPlayfulRollFlip`) and `src/world/panda_genetics_variants.zig` (23 tests: `PandaGenotype`, `PandaBehavior`, `PandaBehaviorState`, `PandaGenetics`, `resolvePhenotype`). Both modules were only referenced via discarded calls in `src/main.zig`.
4. **Sea Turtle & Turtle Shell Helmet**: `src/world/sea_turtle.zig` (18 tests: `TurtleEggClutch`, `tryTrampleTurtleEgg`, `tickEggClutchHatch`, `SeaTurtleState`, `tickBabyTurtleGrowth`) and `src/world/turtle_scute_helmet.zig` (24 tests: `TurtleEntity`, `TurtleEgg`, `feedBabySeagrass`, `tickBabyGrowth`, `craftTurtleHelmet`, `HelmetBreathing` 200-tick / 10s Water Breathing). In `src/main.zig`, `.turtle_helmet` granted infinite water breathing and `active_turtle` growth dropped nothing.

## Decision
1. **Unify the 4 module pairs into their canonical modules**:
   - **Fox (`src/world/taiga_fox.zig`)**: Merged all declarations and tests from `src/world/fox_sweet_berry.zig` (renaming its 2-tag enum to `BerryFoxVariant` to preserve `FoxVariant`'s `u8` discriminants), added `BerryBushTracker` (64-entry bush maturity & regrowth tracker) and `offerMouthItem`, and deleted `src/world/fox_sweet_berry.zig` (59 tests).
   - **Ghast (`src/world/ghast_deflection.zig`)**: Merged all declarations and tests from `src/world/ghast_fireball_tennis.zig` into a unified `GhastFireball` supporting both deflection APIs, added `canMeleeHitFireball`, and deleted `src/world/ghast_fireball_tennis.zig` (67 tests).
   - **Panda (`src/world/panda_genetics.zig`)**: Merged all declarations and tests from `src/world/panda_genetics_variants.zig`, added `ActivePandaState` (`feedBamboo`, `tickSneeze`, `cycleAlleles`), and deleted `src/world/panda_genetics_variants.zig` (40 tests).
   - **Sea Turtle (`src/world/sea_turtle.zig`)**: Merged all declarations and tests from `src/world/turtle_scute_helmet.zig` into unified `GrowthResult` (`matured_to_adult`/`dropped_turtle_scute` and `matured`/`scutes_dropped`), and deleted `src/world/turtle_scute_helmet.zig` (47 tests).
   - Removed all 4 deleted files from `test_files` in `build.zig`, removed their imports from `src/main.zig`, and removed all discarded dummy calls for both the deleted and canonical modules.
2. **Connect all 4 mechanics to live gameplay**:
   - **Entity IDs & Turtle Growth (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_manager.zig`)**: Added `Mob.id`, `Mob.is_baby`, `Mob.growth_timer`, `MobManager.spawnBabyTurtle`, `MobManager.pending_scute_drops`, and `MobManager.tryFeedTurtleSeagrass` (82 `mob_manager` / 450 `entity.zig` tests).
   - **Fox (`src/main.zig`)**: Wired `BerryBushTracker` to F-key `.sweet_berry_bush` harvesting (`harvestBerryBush`, 2–3 berries, stage regression to 1, bone meal fertilization, 15s/stage regrowth). Wired `active_fox` daylight/tree-shade sleep, player wake disturbance, crouch-pounce arc (`computeFoxPounceArc`), snow-stuck pounce landing (`resolvePounceLanding`), Chorus Fruit mouth warp, and right-click feeding/item swap (`offerMouthItem` with `.sweet_berry_fox_treat`, `.sweet_berries`, `.glow_berries`, `.totem_of_undying`, `.emerald`, `.chorus_fruit`).
   - **Ghast Fireball Deflection (`src/main.zig`)**: Recorded `owner_mob_id` on `.hostile_fireball` spawns; connected left-click melee swing deflection (`canMeleeHitFireball` + `deflectGhastFireball`) and player projectile interception (`.arrow`, `.spectral_arrow`, `.snowball`, `.trident`, `.player_fire_charge`), gravity-free reflected flight, lethal mob impact (`resolveFireballImpactOnGhast`) with "Return to Sender" bonus `.ghast_tear` + toast, and block blast-resistance filtering (`canGhastFireballDestroyBlock`).
   - **Panda (`src/main.zig`)**: Connected `.bamboo` right-click breeding (scanning 5m radius for >= 8 `.bamboo` blocks via `canBreedPandasWithBamboo` / `PandaGenetics.breedingState` / `inheritOffspring`), `.panda_gene_scroll` allele/behavior inspection, and baby panda periodic sneezing (`rollBabyPandaSneeze`) with 1-in-700 `.slimeball` drops.
   - **Sea Turtle & Helmet (`src/main.zig`)**: Replaced infinite `.turtle_helmet` water breathing with 20 Hz `HelmetBreathing` tracking (`WATER_BREATHING_TICKS = 200`, 10s Water Breathing on water entry/surface before oxygen depletes underwater), connected `active_turtle` and `.turtle` mob baby-to-adult maturation `.scute` drops, and added right-click `.seagrass` feeding to hatch baby turtles or accelerate baby growth by 10%.
