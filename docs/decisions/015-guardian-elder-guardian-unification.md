# Decision 015: Guardian and Elder Guardian combat unification and gameplay wiring

Accepted: 2026-11-04.

## Context
Guardian, Elder Guardian, and Ocean Monument mechanics were fragmented across three overlapping modules: `src/world/ocean_monument.zig` (which modeled `GuardianLaserState`, `tickGuardianLaserBeam`, `guardianLaserChargeColor`, `evaluateGuardianSpikeThorns`, and `placeSpongeInBiomeOrDimension`), `src/world/guardian_laser_spikes.zig` (which duplicated constants, laser ticks, and spike state), and `src/world/elder_guardian_curse.zig` (which modeled Elder Guardian stats, Mining Fatigue III, and apex vault loot). Meanwhile, `src/main.zig` imported both duplicate modules solely to discard constants (`LASER_CHARGE_TICKS` and `MINING_FATIGUE_III_TICKS`), and ticked `tickGuardianLaserBeam`, `evaluateGuardianSpikeThorns`, and `placeSpongeInBiomeOrDimension` as unconditional dummy calls in the frame loop. Furthermore, live Guardians and Elder Guardians in the world only attacked via close-range melee contact without charging cues or piercing damage, and striking them in melee did not reflect defensive spike thorns damage.

## Decision
1. **Module ownership**: `src/world/ocean_monument.zig` is the single authoritative module for Guardian, Elder Guardian, and Ocean Monument rules. Canonical Minecraft constants (`GUARDIAN_MAX_HEALTH = 30.0`, `ELDER_MAX_HEALTH = 80.0`, `SWIM_SPEED = 2.4`, `SWIM_SPEED_ELDER = 1.8`, `LAND_SPEED = 0.6`, `SPIKE_COUNT = 12`, `THORNS_DAMAGE = 2.0`, `LASER_CHARGE_TICKS = 60`, `CURSE_RADIUS = 50.0`, `MINING_FATIGUE_III_TICKS = 6000`, `PHANTOM_DURATION_TICKS = 30`, `MINING_SPEED_MULTIPLIER = 0.001`, `TIDE_TEMPLATE_CHANCE = 0.20`, `EXPERIENCE_DROP_ELDER = 10`, `APEX_GOLD_BLOCK_COUNT = 8`), room layout (`MONUMENT_GUARDIANS`), curse tracking (`CurseState`), and drop/loot helpers (`guardianDrops`, `defeatElderLoot`, `beamDamage`) were unified into `ocean_monument.zig`. The duplicate modules `src/world/guardian_laser_spikes.zig` and `src/world/elder_guardian_curse.zig` were removed along with their test entries in `build.zig`.
2. **Laser beam combat & visual charging cues**: In `src/main.zig`, the discarded constants and unconditional dummy ticks were removed. When an active Guardian or Elder Guardian mob is within laser range (15m) of the player:
   - `active_guardian_laser` ticks using `ocean_monument_mod.tickGuardianLaserBeam` with line-of-sight tracking and target distance.
   - While charging, visual cues spawn around the charging mob based on `guardianLaserChargeColor`: purple portal particles (`spawnPortalParticles`) and cyan debris, progressing through orange debris and flashing yellow shimmer (`spawnGlowstoneShimmer`).
   - When `fired_beam` is true, the laser applies tiered armor reduction to physical damage and unmitigated armor-piercing magic damage to player health, plays `.splash` and `.mob_roar` audio with hurt feedback, and inflicts Mining Fatigue on the player if the attacker is an Elder Guardian. If no guardian is in range, the laser charge cleanly resets.
3. **Defensive spike thorns reflection**: In `src/main.zig`, when the player strikes a Guardian or Elder Guardian mob in melee with left-click, defensive spike thorns are evaluated via `ocean_monument_mod.evaluateGuardianSpikeThorns(true, true)` (2 damage), damaging the player with `.hurt` audio and orange spike particle feedback (`spawnBlockDebris` with canonical spike color `.{ 1.0, 0.28, 0.06 }`).
