# Session 328: Milestone 210 — Phantom Mob Variant, Insomnia Spawning, Phantom Membrane Drops & Potion of Slow Falling Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the predatory Phantom mob variant (`MobType.phantom`), 5-box midnight-indigo aerial predator geometry with flapping wings and rudder tail, player insomnia mechanics with bed reset, insomnia-triggered nighttime aerial summoning, Phantom Membrane drops (`ItemType.phantom_membrane = 172`), craftable Potion of Slow Falling (`ItemType.slow_falling_potion = 173`), membrane Elytra crafting/repair Recipe 129, and gentle aerial descent physics (-2.0 m/s clamp).

## Changes
- **Player Insomnia & Slow Falling Vitals (`src/player.zig`)**:
  - Added `insomnia_timer: f32 = 0.0` accumulating via `tickVitals(dt)`.
  - Added `resetInsomnia()` which resets on bed sleep and respawn.
  - Added `hasInsomnia()` threshold at 60s wakefulness (~3 Minecraft days equivalent).
  - Added `slow_falling_timer: f32 = 0.0`, `drinkSlowFalling()` (90s duration), and `hasSlowFalling()` query.
  - Integrated slow falling float physics in `tickPhysicsFull`, clamping terminal descent to -2.0 m/s without fall damage.
- **Phantom Mob Stats & 3D Flight Kinematics (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - `Mob.init`: 20 HP, 0.9m width, 0.5m height, 4.2 m/s flight speed, sound `.mob_roar`.
  - Circling aerial patrol kinematics at y=12..16 with harmonic sinusoidal altitude modulation.
  - 5-box aerial predator geometry: fuselage body, green-eyed head, 2 flapping articulated wings, trailing rudder tail.
- **Items & Crafting Recipes (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.phantom_membrane = 172` (stack cap 64) and `ItemType.slow_falling_potion = 173` (stack cap 16).
  - Mob defeat meat drop: `.phantom => .phantom_membrane`.
  - Recipe 128: 1 Phantom Membrane + 1 Glass vial (or Nether Wart) -> 1 Slow Falling Potion.
  - Recipe 129: 2 Phantom Membranes + 2 Strings/Purpur -> 1 Elytra.
  - Comprehensive unit test covering recipes, stack caps, drops, and item names.
- **Nighttime Insomnia Spawning & Hostility (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Nighttime check summons circling Phantoms above players experiencing insomnia.
  - Bed sleep handler resets `player.resetInsomnia()`.
  - Consumable right-click handler for `slow_falling_potion` activates `player.drinkSlowFalling()` with particle burst.
  - Added Phantom swoop attack dealing 3 damage with aerial roar audio in hostile combat loop.
