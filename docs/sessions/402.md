# Session 402: Milestone 483 - Zombified Piglin Neutrality & Group Alarm Swarm

## Goal
Implement Milestone 483: Zombified Piglin Neutrality & Group Alarm Swarm in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/zombified_piglin_swarm.zig`.
- **Zombified Piglin Neutrality & Anger State Machine**:
  - `ZombifiedPiglinState`: `.neutral`, `.angered`, `.calming`.
  - Max Health: **20.0 HP** (10 hearts) (`MAX_HEALTH = 20.0`, `HEARTS = 10.0`, `ZOMBIFIED_PIGLIN_MAX_HEALTH = 20.0`).
  - Natural Neutrality: Passively ignores players under normal conditions, wandering calmly in the Nether at 3.0 m/s neutral walk speed (`NEUTRAL_WALK_SPEED = 3.0`).
  - Anger trigger: Hostile towards players only when struck or damaged (`angerAt(player_id)`), or when alerted by group alarm. Transitions to `.angered`, assigns `target_player_id`, applies +45% speed boost, samples anger timer, and emits screech.
- **40-Block Group Alarm Swarm Propagation**:
  - Emits piercing screech audio cue (`SoundEffect.zombified_piglin_screech`) when provoked or attacked.
  - Broadcasts alarm across a **40.0m Euclidean radius** (`SWARM_ALARM_RADIUS = 40.0`, `SWARM_ALARM_RADIUS_SQ = 1600.0`).
  - Line-of-sight check is NOT required: screech penetrates solid walls, netherrack, ceilings, and caves.
  - Every alerted Zombified Piglin within 40.0m enters `.angered` targeting the provoker player (`target_player_id = player_id`) and sprints at 4.35 m/s. Out-of-range piglins (> 40.0m) remain neutral.
- **Anger Duration & Calming Cycle**:
  - Anger timer: uniformly sampled between **400 and 799 game ticks** (20.0 to 39.95 seconds) (`MIN_ANGER_TICKS = 400`, `MAX_ANGER_TICKS = 799`).
  - Speed boost while angered: movement speed increases by +45% from 3.0 m/s to **4.35 m/s** (`ANGERED_SPRINT_SPEED = 4.35`).
  - Continuous pursuit re-aggro: while a target player remains within line-of-sight and within **16.0m Euclidean radius** (`RE_AGGRO_RADIUS = 16.0`), the anger timer resets, maintaining relentless pursuit.
  - Calming: when anger timer reaches 0 without seeing the player, transitions to `.calming` (for 20 ticks / 1.0s), then returns to `.neutral` with `target_player_id = null` and neutral 3.0 m/s speed (anger forgiven).
- **Undead Fire & Lava Immunity & Reinforcement Summoning**:
  - Undead fire immunity: completely immune to fire, lava, and burning damage (`isImmuneToLava = true`, `isImmuneToFire = true`, `is_undead = true`), taking 0.0 damage and ignoring environmental fire.
  - Reinforcement calling: taking damage has a **10% chance** (`REINFORCEMENT_CHANCE = 0.10`) to summon another Zombified Piglin within **16 blocks** (`REINFORCEMENT_RADIUS = 16.0`), which immediately enters `.angered` targeting the attacker.
- **Drops on Defeat**:
  - Rotten Flesh (0–1 base, scaled by Looting).
  - Gold Nuggets (0–1 base, scaled by Looting).
  - Golden Sword: **8.5% base chance** (`GOLDEN_SWORD_BASE_DROP_CHANCE = 0.085`), increasing by +1.0% per Looting level.
  - Experience Points: **5 XP** (`EXPERIENCE_POINTS_DROP = 5`).
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/zombified_piglin_swarm.zig` in `build.zig` `test_files`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Implemented 44 comprehensive unit tests covering neutrality, anger triggers, 40m swarm alarm propagation, out-of-range immunity, anger duration countdown and calming, LOS pursuit re-aggro, lava/fire immunity, reinforcement calling, defeat drops, and combat encounter simulation.
