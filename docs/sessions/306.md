# Session 306: Milestone 188 — Zombie Mob Variant, Rotten Flesh Drops, Hunger Debuff Pipeline & Rare Iron Ingot Drops

## Summary
Implemented the iconic Zombie mob variant (`MobType.zombie`), Rotten Flesh item (`ItemType.rotten_flesh = 145`), accelerated hunger depletion debuff (`player.hunger_debuff_timer = 15.0`), 6-box humanoid renderer mesh with arms outstretched forward, rare iron ingot loot roll (`dropsZombieIron`), and cave/night hostile spawning.

## Changes
- **Item & Player Debuff (`src/inventory.zig`, `src/player.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.rotten_flesh = 145` (HUD tint `(0.60, 0.38, 0.22, 0.95)`, compostable in composter).
  - Added `MobType.zombie` dropping `.rotten_flesh` on defeat.
  - Added `dropsZombieIron(wx, wy, wz)` deterministic 12.5% (~1-in-8) iron ingot drop chance.
  - Added player hunger debuff pipeline (`hunger_debuff_timer`, `applyHungerDebuff()`, `hasHungerDebuff()`): accelerates hunger drain rate by +0.20/s during the 15-second duration.
  - Golden apple and respawn cleanse hunger debuff.
  - Unit tests: verified rotten flesh item properties, compostability, iron drop roll, and player hunger debuff accelerated drain.
- **Mob Attributes & Renderer (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Configured zombie mob attributes: 10 HP, 0.6m width, 1.8m height, 2.2 m/s speed, `.mob_growl` sound.
  - Built 6-box zombie mesh geometry: cyan/teal torso shirt, moss green zombie face skull, two arms outstretched forward (0.12x0.12x0.52), and two legs with walking gait swing (144 verts, 216 inds).
  - Unit tests: verified zombie dimensions, mob audio, and renderer vertex/index counts.
- **Spawning, Combat & Ingestion (`src/entity/mob_manager.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Updated cave and darkness night spawning to include zombies (`roll_m < 0.28 => .zombie`).
  - Integrated zombies into pack radius hunting AI and repulsion checks.
  - Zombie melee bite inflicts the hunger debuff on the player.
  - Ingesting rotten flesh restores 4 hunger points while inflicting the hunger debuff with brown debris VFX.
  - Defeating zombies awards rotten flesh and rolls for rare iron ingot drops.
