# Session 391: Milestone 470 - Archaeology Brush Excavation & Suspicious Substrate Extraction

## Goal
Implement Milestone 470: Archaeology Brush Excavation & Suspicious Substrate Extraction in Zigcraft as a modular, fully tested world subsystem.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/archaeology_brush_excavation.zig`.
- **Brush Tool Mechanics**:
  - Durability: max durability 64 (`ItemType.brush`).
  - Crafting: 1 Feather + 1 Copper Ingot + 1 Stick in vertical column format (`craftBrushVertical`, `craftBrush3x3` supporting left, center, and right columns).
  - Durability consumption: consumes 1 durability point upon completed excavation (96 ticks) or interrupted brushing.
  - Brushing duration: 4.8 seconds (96 game ticks / 24 brushing cycles of 4 ticks).
- **Suspicious Substrates**:
  - `SuspiciousBlockType`: `suspicious_sand` (Desert Wells, Desert Pyramids, Warm Ocean Ruins) and `suspicious_gravel` (Cold Ocean Ruins, Trail Ruins).
  - Fragile physics: Suspicious blocks fall with gravity, but shatter instantly on fall impact, dropping zero items (zero loot, destroyed artifact).
  - Mining: Breaking with any tool drops normal sand or gravel (artifact permanently destroyed).
- **4 Dust Stages & Visual Progression**:
  - Stage 0: 0% uncovered (0-23 ticks).
  - Stage 1: 25% uncovered (24-47 ticks, initial dust puff, top tip of artifact visible).
  - Stage 2: 50% uncovered (48-71 ticks, heavy dust puff, half of artifact visible).
  - Stage 3: 75% uncovered (72-95 ticks, continuous dust plume, artifact almost completely exposed).
  - Stage 4 / Complete: 100% uncovered (96 ticks, artifact fully extruded, converts block to normal sand or gravel).
  - Brushing Decay: Releasing right-click before completion decays progress by 1 stage every 40 ticks (2.0s) until stage 0.
- **Archaeological Loot Extraction**:
  - Loot pools: 20 Pottery Sherds, Sniffer Egg (6.7% in warm ocean ruins), Trail Ruins Armor Trim Templates (Wayfinder, Raiser, Shaper, Host), Emerald, Wheat, Coal.
  - Extrudes `ArtifactEntity` at block center `(x+0.5, y+0.5, z+0.5)` with upward pop velocity (`vy = 0.2`).
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/archaeology_brush_excavation.zig` in `build.zig`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - 32 comprehensive unit tests in `src/world/archaeology_brush_excavation.zig` verifying crafting, durability, physics, dust stages, decay, and loot tables.
