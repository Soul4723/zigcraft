## Goal
Implement Milestone 156: Hopper Block Crafting & Autonomous Item Siphoning Pipeline.   - Added Recipe 71: 1 Chest + 1..2 Iron Ingots -> 1 Hopper.
  - Added unit test covering properties, roundtrips, and crafting recipes.
- `src/main.zig`:
  - Added `HopperState` tracking placed hoppers, 0.4s cooldown timer, and 4 internal item slots.
  - Wired placement hook when putting down hoppers.
  - Implemented hopper item transfer pipeline:
    - Siphons items from chest containers directly above (`y + 1`) into internal hopper storage.
    - Funnels items downward into chest containers directly below (`y - 1`).
    - Redstone power input (`isDispenserPowered`) actively locks hopper, preventing extraction and transfer.
    - Plays click sound and spawns iron debris particles upon item transfer.
