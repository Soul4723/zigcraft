# Session 333: Milestone 215 — Trident Riptide Water Propulsion & Aquatic Spiral Vortex Mechanics

## Summary
Implemented the iconic Trident Riptide propulsion mechanic (`ItemType.trident`), allowing players submerged or wading in water to launch themselves rapidly in their look direction with a swirling cyan-aquamarine vortex spiral particle effect, custom acoustics, tool wear degradation, and directional velocity boost.

## Changes
- **Player Mechanics (`src/player.zig`)**:
  - Added `riptide_timer: f32 = 0.0` (duration 0.6s) to `Player`.
  - Added `launchRiptide()`, `isRiptiding() bool`, and vitals decay in `tickVitals`.
  - Added `riptide_speed_scale: f32 = 5.5` to horizontal movement physics in `tickPhysicsFull`.
- **Particle System (`src/renderer/particles.zig`)**:
  - Added `spawnRiptideSpiral(pos, count)` emitting a spiraling, double-helix tangential vortex of cyan-aquamarine oceanic foam particles around the player.
- **Controls & Activation (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Right-click with held Trident:
    - If player is in water or eye is underwater, triggers `player.launchRiptide()`.
    - Imparts high launch impulse: `vel_y = cam.front().y * 14.0 + 4.0` along with 5.5x horizontal speed scale.
    - Consumes 1 durability from held Trident via `tool_wear.use`.
    - Spawns 16 riptide spiral particles and 12 water splash particles.
    - Plays dual `.splash` and `.mob_teleport` audio cues and shows toast notification.
