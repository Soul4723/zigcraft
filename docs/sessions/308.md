# Session 308: Milestone 190 — Enderman Mob Variant, Ender Pearl Thrown Teleportation & Eerie Acoustics

## Summary
Implemented the iconic Enderman mob variant (`MobType.enderman`), Ender Pearl item (`ItemType.ender_pearl = 147`), `.mob_teleport` synthesized pitch-swept whoosh pop audio, 6-box tall slender humanoid mesh geometry (2.9m height with 1.4m limbs), thrown projectile ballistics with purple vortex particle trail, player impact teleportation with fall damage mitigation, and hostile cave/night spawner integration.

## Changes
- **Audio Synthesizer (`src/audio/synth.zig`)**:
  - Added `.mob_teleport` to `SoundType` (~330ms duration).
  - Synthesized eerie pitch-bent sweep (880Hz down to 220Hz) modulated with shimmer noise and quadratic envelope.
  - Unit test: `enderman mob_teleport audio synthesis`.
- **Item & Inventory (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.ender_pearl = 147` (stack cap 16, HUD tint `0.12, 0.45, 0.42, 0.95`).
  - Added `MobType.enderman` dropping `.ender_pearl` via `getMobMeatDrop(.enderman) = .ender_pearl`.
  - Unit test: `enderman mob drop, ender pearl item properties, and stack cap`.
- **Mob Attributes & Renderer (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Configured enderman mob attributes: 20 HP, 0.6m width, 2.9m height, 3.2 m/s walk speed, `.mob_teleport` sound.
  - Built 6-box tall slender mesh geometry: jet-black obsidian torso (0.36x0.72x0.20), purple-eyed skull (0.32 cube), two thin 1.4m arms (0.08x1.40x0.08), and two thin 1.44m legs (0.09x1.44x0.09) with reciprocal walking swing (144 verts, 216 inds).
  - Unit tests: verified enderman stats, audio mapping, and mesh geometry vertex/index counts.
- **Spawner & Teleportation Gameplay (`src/entity/mob_manager.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Cave and night darkness spawning incorporates endermen (`roll_m < 0.20 => .enderman`).
  - Added `ender_pearl` to `ThrownKind` projectile system.
  - Key `G` throws held ender pearls with purple portal particle burst and `.mob_teleport` sound.
  - Projectile flight emits purple portal shimmer particles.
  - Block impact instantaneously teleports player to collision coordinates, resets vertical velocity, applies 5.0 fall damage (mitigated by netherite boots), plays 3D spatial `.mob_teleport` audio and hurt feedback, and spawns 24 portal particles.
  - Enderman melee attack strikes player for 4 damage (mitigated by armor).
