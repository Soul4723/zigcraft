# Session 396: Milestone 476 - Riptide Trident Water/Rain Kinematics & Kinetic Impact

## Goal
Implement Milestone 476: Riptide Trident Water/Rain Kinematics & Kinetic Impact in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/riptide_trident_kinematics.zig`.
- **Riptide Enchantment Prerequisite & Environment Validation**:
  - Implemented `RiptideLevel` enum supporting Level I, Level II, and Level III with integer conversion and launch speed scaling.
  - Implemented `PlayerEnvironment` validating environment prerequisites:
    - Standing in water (`WaterMedium.still_water`, `flowing_water`, `bubble_column`) OR
    - Exposed to rain/thunderstorm under open sky (`weather == .raining or .thunderstorming` and `exposed_to_sky == true`).
    - Dry weather while outside water strictly fails with `CanRiptideResult.not_in_water_or_rain`.
    - Sheltered/indoor locations during rain without water strictly fail with `CanRiptideResult.not_in_water_or_rain`.
    - Submerged/standing in water indoors succeeds regardless of overhead block coverage.
- **Mutual Exclusivity Enforcements**:
  - Enforced strict mutual exclusivity between Riptide and Loyalty / Channeling enchantments.
  - Tridents containing both Riptide and (Loyalty or Channeling) fail validation with `CanRiptideResult.incompatible_enchantments`.
  - Non-conflicting enchantments (Unbreaking, Mending, Impaling) are fully permitted alongside Riptide.
- **Throw Charge & Launch Kinematics**:
  - Charge duration tracking: minimum threshold of 10 ticks (0.50s), full charge of 20 ticks (1.0s). Attempts released prior to 10 ticks fail with `CanRiptideResult.insufficient_charge`.
  - Directional launch velocity along camera look vector (supports direct 3D vector or pitch/yaw angle conversion):
    `initial_speed = 6.0 + 3.0 * level m/s`:
    - Level I: 9.0 m/s
    - Level II: 12.0 m/s
    - Level III: 15.0 m/s
  - Hydrodynamic vortex animation: activates spinning state (`is_spinning = true`) with a 20-tick flight duration countdown.
  - Generates swirling hydrodynamic vortex trail particles (`ParticleType.bubble` and `ParticleType.splash`) with spiral angular displacement and tangential velocity offsets.
- **Kinetic Collision & Radial AOE Impact**:
  - Direct collision damage: deals base Trident melee damage (9.0 HP / 4.5 hearts) + kinetic velocity scaling (`bonus = speed * 0.5 HP`).
  - Radial AOE splash damage: deals 9.0 HP splash damage to all hostile mobs within 2.0m radius of the impact center point.
  - Outward radial knockback impulse: calculates normalized 3D impulse directed outward away from player origin.
  - Primary target receives direct kinetic scaled damage and is not duplicated in AOE splash.
  - Passive mobs and non-hostile entities are excluded from hostile AOE splash.
- **Fall Damage Mitigation**:
  - Launching Riptide resets downward vertical fall distance to 0 (`fall_distance = 0.0`), allowing vertical, horizontal, or diagonal surges to negate all accumulated fall damage.
- **Durability Wear**:
  - Consumes exactly 1 durability point on the Trident per Riptide launch.
  - Broken tridents (0 durability) cannot activate Riptide (`CanRiptideResult.broken_trident`).
- **Build Registration & Testing**:
  - Registered `src/world/riptide_trident_kinematics.zig` in `build.zig`.
  - Implemented 49 unit tests covering all prerequisites, charge thresholds, speed scaling, trajectory calculations, spin state, particle generation, kinetic damage breakdown, AOE splash radius, radial knockback, mutual exclusivity, fall damage reset, durability wear, and full integration simulation.
