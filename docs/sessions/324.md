## Summary
Implemented Dragon's Breath item (`ItemType.dragons_breath = 165`), Lingering Potions of Poison and Weakness (`ItemType.lingering_poison_potion = 166`, `ItemType.lingering_weakness_potion = 167`), Recipes 122–124, dynamic Area of Effect `VaporCloud` hazard simulation (`src/world/hazard.zig`), Ender Dragon periodic strafe spewing 4.0m breath clouds, glass bottle collection of Dragon's Breath, and deployable lingering potion clouds with status affliction.

  - Added Recipe 122: 1 Glass (vial) + 1 Dragon Head / Chorus Fruit -> 1 Dragon's Breath.
  - Added Recipe 123: 1 Dragon's Breath + 1 Poison Potion -> 1 Lingering Poison Potion.
  - Added Recipe 124: 1 Dragon's Breath + 1 Weakness Potion -> 1 Lingering Weakness Potion.
  - Unit tests: verified item roundtrips, stack limits, names, and crafting recipes 122–124.
- **Dragon Strafe Attack & Cloud Mechanics (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Ender Dragon periodic strafe attack: spawns 4.0m radius Dragon's Breath hazard clouds at `y = 8.5` with `.dragon_roar` audio.
  - Vapor cloud physics loop: decays cloud timers, emits swirling colored mist particles, applies 2.0 dmg/s from dragon's breath, and inflicts poison or weakness debuffs on players standing within cloud radius.
  - Dragon's Breath bottling: right-clicking with a glass bottle inside a dragon breath cloud consumes 1 glass and awards 1 Dragon's Breath with splash audio.
  - Lingering potion deployment: right-clicking with lingering poison or weakness potions deploys a 3.5m radius vapor cloud at the targeted location with vortex particles and sizzle audio.
