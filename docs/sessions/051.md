## Goal

Add Redstone Torch block acting as both a constant redstone power emitter and an inverter (NOT logic gate), with burnt/glowing pixel textures and redstone dust + stick crafting recipe.

## What was done

- `src/world/block.zig`:
  - Added `redstone_torch_off = 37` and `redstone_torch_on = 38` block IDs.
  - Transparent, non-solid pass-through, instant break hardness (0.0).
  - Atlas mappings: torch off (6, 7), torch on (7, 7).
  - 1 new unit test validating solidity, transparency, and face textures.
- `src/renderer/texture.zig`:
  - Atlas (6, 7): wooden shaft with dark crimson unlit redstone tip.
  - Atlas (7, 7): wooden shaft with bright orange-red spark center and glowing vermilion electric aura.
  - 1 new unit test verifying transparent margin, dark vs radiant tips, and wooden shaft.
- `src/inventory.zig`:
  - Added `ItemType.redstone_torch = 43`.
  - Recipe 26: 1 Redstone Dust vertically above 1 Stick -> 1 Redstone Torch.
  - 1 new unit test verifying crafting output.
- `src/main.zig`:
  - Placed redstone torches emit constant power to adjacent wires, repeaters, lamps, and doors.
  - NOT gate logic: actively monitors supporting block underneath; when the supporting block is powered by wires/levers/plates, the torch inverts and extinguishes (`redstone_torch_off`), cutting downstream power.
