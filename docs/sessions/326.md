# Session 326: Milestone 208 — Turtle Mob Variant, Scute Drops, Turtle Shell Helmet & Water Breathing Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the amphibious Turtle mob variant (`MobType.turtle`), 6-box marine reptile rendering geometry with flipper paddle animation, Scute mob drop (`ItemType.scute = 168`), craftable Turtle Shell Helmet (`ItemType.turtle_helmet = 169`), craftable Water Breathing Potion (`ItemType.water_breathing_potion = 170`), and water breathing respiratory protection pipeline.

## Changes
- **Vitals & Hazard Pipeline (`src/player.zig`, `src/world/hazard.zig`)**:
  - Added `water_breathing_timer: f32` to Player struct with `drinkWaterBreathing()` (180s duration) and `hasWaterBreathing()` query.
  - Vitals decay and respawn reset for water breathing status.
  - Updated `tickOxygenRespiration` in `hazard.zig` to accept `has_water_breathing: bool`, bypassing underwater oxygen drain when active.
- **Items, Mob Type & Crafting (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `MobType.turtle` to `MobType` enum; drops `ItemType.scute` on defeat.
  - Added items:
    - `ItemType.scute = 168` (stack cap 64, scute green color).
    - `ItemType.turtle_helmet = 169` (stack cap 1, durability 275, wearable armor).
    - `ItemType.water_breathing_potion = 170` (stack cap 16, cyan/teal potion color).
  - Added Recipes:
    - Recipe 125: 4 Scutes (2x2 craft grid) -> 1 Turtle Shell Helmet.
    - Recipe 126: 1 Scute + 1 Glass vial (or Water / Nether Wart) -> 1 Water Breathing Potion.
  - Comprehensive unit test covering recipes, tool properties, stack limits, and drops.
- **Mob Physics & Geometry (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - `Mob.init`: 30 HP, 0.9m width, 0.4m height, 0.8 m/s walk speed on land, sound `.mob_growl`.
  - In `Mob.tick`: dynamic swim speed boost to 2.8 m/s while inside water.
  - 6-box amphibious geometry in `mob_renderer.zig`: wide domed carapace shell, head with bobbing animation, 4 articulated flipper paddles swinging in opposition.
- **Main Game Loop (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Player oxygen tick checks `player.hasWaterBreathing() or inventory.hasItem(.turtle_helmet)` for complete underwater drowning prevention.
  - Consumable right-click handler for `water_breathing_potion`: triggers `player.drinkWaterBreathing()`, water splash particle burst, and audio feedback.

- Binary compilation: builds cleanly without errors (`zig build`).
