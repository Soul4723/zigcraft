## What Was Done

### 1. Milestone 299 — Armadillos, Scutes & Dyable Wolf Armor (`src/world/armadillo_wolf_armor.zig`)
- **Threat Detection & Roll-Up State Machine**: Armadillos detect threats within 7.0m (`threat_detection_radius`), transitioning through `unrolled -> rolling_up (0.75s) -> rolled_up (3.0s) -> peeking (2.0s) -> unrolling (0.75s)`. In the rolled-up state, attacks under 2.0 damage are completely resisted and higher attacks undergo Minecraft scaling reduction.
- **Scute Brushing**: Brushing an unrolled or rolled armadillo yields 1 Armadillo Scute (`ItemType.armadillo_scute = 413`) and consumes 16 durability from the brush.
- **Wolf Armor Absorption**: Crafts from 4 scutes (`Recipe 297`) with 64 max durability (`ItemType.wolf_armor = 414`). Absorbs 100% of non-environmental damage (melee, projectile, explosion) until shattered, exhibiting 3 crack stages (thresholds 60, 44, 20).
- **Repairing & Dyeing**: Armadillo scutes restore 8 durability each up to 64. Wolf armor supports 16 custom dye colors and dye washing. Spiders flee from unrolled armadillos within 6.0m.

### 2. Milestone 300 — Bogged & Ominous Trials (`src/world/ominous_trial.zig`)
- **Bogged Mob Variant**: Moss-grown swamp skeleton with 16.0 HP, slower firing interval (3.5s vs 2.0s), and 4.0s poison-tipped arrows. Shearing drops 2 mushrooms (red or brown).
- **Ominous Bottle & Omen Conversion**: `ItemType.ominous_bottle = 415` grants Bad Omen (6000s). Entering a 14.0m radius of a Trial Spawner converts Bad Omen into Trial Omen (`900s * amplifier`).
- **Ominous Trial Spawner**: Upgrades normal trial spawners into ominous mode, doubling mob armor equip chance (50%), spawning periodic projectile/potion hazards every 8.0s, and dispensing Ominous Trial Keys (`ItemType.ominous_trial_key = 416`, Recipe 298) at a 30% drop probability.
- **Ominous Vault**: `BlockId.ominous_vault = 250` (emissive 0.85 cyan soul flame glow, Recipe 299). Unlocks exclusively with Ominous Trial Keys, dispensing top-tier loot (Mace, Enchanted Golden Apple, Music Discs Precipice/Creator, Wind Charges).

### 3. Milestone 301 — Tricky Trials Alchemy (`src/world/trial_alchemy.zig`)
- **Trial Potions**: Added 4 distinct combat and mobility potions with 180s duration (`ItemType` 418–421, Recipe 300):
  - **Potion of Infested**: 10% chance on entity hurt to burst forth 1–2 silverfish.
  - **Potion of Oozing**: Spawns 2 medium slimes in a 5x5x5 volume centered on death location.
  - **Potion of Weaving**: Doubles cobweb traversal speed (0.25 -> 0.50) and leaves 2–3 cobwebs upon death.
  - **Potion of Wind Charged**: Emits an upward wind burst impulse (vy = +6.5) and radial knockback impulse within 3.5m upon death.

### 4. Milestone 302 — Bundles of Bravery (`src/world/bundle_pouch.zig`)
- **64-Unit Weight Model**: `ItemType.bundle = 422` (stack 1, Recipe 301: 1 String + 1 Leather).
  - 64-stack items occupy 1 weight unit each.
  - 16-stack items occupy 4 weight units each.
  - 1-stack items occupy the full 64 weight units.
  - Nested empty bundles occupy 4 weight units.
- **Pouch Storage & Selection**: Tracks up to 16 distinct item entries. Supports LIFO extraction or mouse-wheel scroll selection cycling, bulk extraction (`emptyAll`), fullness bar UI calculation, and comparator fullness signal (0..15). Supports 16 dye colors.

### 5. Milestone 303 — Copper Fixtures & Oxidation Parity (`src/world/copper_fixtures.zig`)
- **Copper Doors & Trapdoors**: `BlockId.copper_door_closed = 246`, `copper_door_open = 247`, `copper_trapdoor_closed = 248`, `copper_trapdoor_open = 249` (Recipe 302: 3 Copper Ingots -> 1 Copper Door; Recipe 303: 1 Copper Door -> 2 Copper Trapdoors, or 2 Copper Ingots horizontally).
- **Interaction Mechanisms**:
  - Direct bare-hand right-click toggles open/closed (unlike iron doors).
  - Redstone rising edge opens and falling edge closes.
  - Wind charge burst impacts trigger an immediate toggle.
- **Oxidation Lifecycle & Maintenance**: 4 stages (`unoxidized`, `exposed`, `weathered`, `oxidized`) progressing every 45s; Honeycomb waxing locks oxidation; Axe scraping strips wax before regressing oxidation stage. Copper bulb T-flip-flop features stage-dependent light emission (15, 12, 8, 4).

### 6. Milestone 304 — Jukebox Music Discs (`src/world/jukebox_discs.zig`)
- **Music Discs**: `ItemType.disc_fragment_5 = 425`, `music_disc_5 = 426`, `music_disc_relic = 427`, `music_disc_precipice = 428`, `music_disc_creator = 429`.
- **Comparator Output**: 15 canonical discs output signals 1..15, modern additions map to exact values (Disc 5 = 15, Relic = 15, Precipice = 13, Creator = 12).
- **Disc 5 Assembly**: Recipe 304 crafts 1 Music Disc 5 from 4 (or 2) Disc Fragments (5).
- **Allay Musical Duplication**: When an Allay is within 10.0m of an actively playing jukebox and offered an Amethyst Shard, it performs a duplication dance and spawns a clone (300s cooldown).
