# Session 305: Milestone 187 — Skeleton Mob Variant, Bow Archery AI, Bone Drops & Bone Meal Milling

## Summary
Implemented the Skeleton mob variant (`MobType.skeleton`), bone item drops (`ItemType.bone = 144`), bone meal milling recipe (Recipe 106: 1 Bone -> 3 Bone Meal), 7-box segmented skeleton renderer mesh with raised bow arm, ranged archery combat AI (1.8s shot cadence within 16m), hostile arrow projectile mechanics with shield blocking, and night/cave spawner integration.

## Changes
- **Item & Crafting (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.bone = 144` with ivory HUD tint `(0.88, 0.86, 0.80, 0.95)`.
  - Recipe 106: 1 Bone -> 3 Bone Meal (`ItemType.bone_meal = 128`).
  - Added `MobType.skeleton` with default bone meat drop via `getMobMeatDrop(.skeleton) = .bone`.
  - Added `dropsSkeletonArrow(wx, wy, wz)` deterministic 50% arrow drop roll for defeated skeletons.
  - Unit tests: verified bone stack size, recipe output, item properties, and arrow drop distribution.
- **Mob Attributes & Spawning (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_manager.zig`)**:
  - Configured skeleton mob statistics: 10 HP, 0.6m width, 1.8m height, 2.4 m/s walk speed, `.mob_growl` acoustic sound effect.
  - Cave and night spawner tables updated to spawn skeletons (`roll_m < 0.35 => .skeleton`).
  - Skeleton mob integrated into pack radius hunting AI and repulsion checks.
  - Unit tests: verified skeleton mob initialization, dimensions, and sound.
- **Renderer (`src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Built 7-box skeleton mesh geometry: ribcage, skull (head bob), left arm (walk swing), right arm raised forward holding wooden bow stave, bow model, and two legs (reciprocal swing).
  - Total 168 vertices, 252 indices matching box mesh geometry.
  - Unit test: verified skeleton vertex and index counts.
- **Combat & Archery AI (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `hostile_arrow` variant to `ThrownKind` projectile system.
  - Skeleton AI: when within 16m of player (and at least 2m away), skeletons fire hostile arrows with an upward loft trajectory every 1.8s.
  - Arrow collision handling: deals 2 damage on impact (mitigated by netherite armor), blocked by active shield with `.shield_block` audio and shield durability consumption, embeds into terrain with `.step_wood` audio.
  - Skeleton defeat handling: yields Bone and 50% chance for 2 reserve Arrows.

## Verification
- Unit test suite: 1756 tests passing cleanly across all files (`zig build test`).
- Binary build: compiles without errors (`zig build`).
