## What was done

- Implemented `src/world/trial_spawner_waves.zig` featuring:
  - **Dynamic Player Scaling Mob Wave Algorithm**:
    - Evaluates nearby survival/adventure players within a 14.0m Euclidean radius ($dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2 \le 196.0$).
    - Total mob wave size scaling formula:
      `N = @intFromFloat(@floor(base_total_mobs * (1.0 + 0.5 * (P - 1))))`
      (e.g., base 6: 1 player -> 6 mobs, 2 players -> 9 mobs, 3 players -> 12 mobs, 4 players -> 15 mobs, 5 players -> 18 mobs).
    - Simultaneous mob cap scaling:
      `M = @intFromFloat(@floor(base_simultaneous * (1.0 + 0.5 * (P - 1))))`
      (e.g., base 2: 1 player -> 2 mobs, 2 players -> 3 mobs, 3 players -> 4 mobs, 4 players -> 5 mobs, 5 players -> 6 mobs).
    - Excludes Creative and Spectator mode players from activation and scaling.
  - **Ominous Spawner Transformation & Equipment Assignment**:
    - Transforms into Ominous Trial Spawner (`is_ominous = true`) when approached by an eligible player with the `trial_omen` effect.
    - Spawns mobs equipped with enchanted armor (Chainmail or Iron armor with Protection I) and enchanted weapons (Sharpness I iron sword or Power I bow).
  - **8.0-Second (160 Ticks) Ominous Hazard Timer**:
    - Ticks down on a 160-tick cadence during active ominous waves.
    - Randomly drops lingering potion clouds (Poison, Slowness, Weaving, Oozing, Infested, Wind Charged) or downward projectile drops (Fire Charges, Wind Charges) directly above target players (3.5m above player with downward velocity).
  - **Ejection & 30-Minute Cooldown**:
    - On wave completion when all spawned mobs are defeated, transitions to `waiting_for_reward_ejection`.
    - Ejects loot bundles with 50% Trial Key probability for standard trials and 30% Ominous Key probability for ominous trials.
    - Locks spawner into a 30-minute (36,000-tick) cooldown venting grey smoke particles (`venting_grey_smoke_particles = true`).
  - **Integration & Unit Testing**:
    - Added 47 unit tests covering scaling formulas, detection boundaries, player eligibility, ominous transformation, enchanted equipment, hazard timing and variety, reward ejection, and cooldown lock behavior.
    - Registered `src/world/trial_spawner_waves.zig` in `build.zig` and connected representative frame-loop queries in `src/main.zig`.
