# Session 392: Milestone 471 - Trial Chambers Spawner Ominous Potion Spawning & Projectile Traps

## Goal
Implement Milestone 471: Trial Chambers Spawner Ominous Potion Spawning & Projectile Traps in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/trial_chamber_traps.zig`.
- **Ominous Spawner Hazard Timers**:
  - Implemented 8.0-second (160 game ticks at 20 TPS) ambient hazard timer (`HAZARD_INTERVAL_TICKS = 160`).
  - Active and ominous trial spawner decrements the hazard timer every tick and triggers hazards on expiration.
  - Automatically resets timer to 160 ticks on trigger and supports multiple periodic cycles.
  - Random hazard selection chooses between:
    1. Ceiling projectile drop (Fire Charge or Wind Charge dropped straight down above player coordinates).
    2. Lingering potion cloud spawn (spawned 3–5 blocks directly above player head).
- **Lingering Potion Hazard Payload Types**:
  - Defined all 6 potion types with canonical duration and effect characteristics:
    - `.poison`: Poison I, 4s duration (80 ticks), periodic poison damage.
    - `.slowness`: Slowness I, 8s duration (160 ticks), 15% speed reduction.
    - `.weaving`: Weaving effect, 12s duration (240 ticks), doubles cobweb traversal speed to 0.50x, spawns 2-3 cobwebs on death.
    - `.oozing`: Oozing effect, 12s duration (240 ticks), spawns 2 medium slimes on death.
    - `.infested`: Infested effect, 12s duration (240 ticks), 10% chance to proc 1-2 silverfish on hurt.
    - `.wind_charged`: Wind Charged effect, 12s duration (240 ticks), triggers 3.5m burst with 6.5 vy upward impulse on death.
- **Lingering Cloud Physical Dynamics**:
  - Initial radius starts at 3.0 meters (`initial_radius = 3.0`, diameter 6.0m).
  - Shrinks by 0.5m every time an entity enters and is afflicted.
  - Dissipates completely when radius reaches 0.0m (after 6 entity afflictions).
  - Maximum unconsumed lifetime: 600 ticks (30.0 seconds).
  - Full spherical radius collision testing (`dx^2 + dy^2 + dz^2 <= radius^2`) with multi-entity support.
- **Ceiling Projectile Ejection & Ground Impact**:
  - Ejection velocity: strictly downward at `[3]f32{ 0.0, -0.6, 0.0 }`.
  - Ground collision detection stops projectiles at ground level.
  - Wind charge ground impact:
    - Triggers 3.5m burst radius.
    - Applies radial horizontal knockback (6.0m impulse) and upward velocity (6.5 vy) to entities within burst radius.
    - Toggles copper doors and copper trapdoors within the 3.5m burst radius (open <-> closed).
  - Fire charge ground impact:
    - Sets fire to flammable blocks (wood, wood planks, leaves, wool, soul sand).
    - Ignites regular fire on combustible blocks, and soul fire on soul sand.
    - Does not ignite non-flammable blocks (stone, deepslate, tuff, copper).
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/trial_chamber_traps.zig` in `build.zig`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Added 37 comprehensive unit tests covering all hazard intervals, selection rolls, potion dynamics, cloud shrinkage, timeout, projectile physics, wind charge burst, door toggles, and fire charge ignition.
