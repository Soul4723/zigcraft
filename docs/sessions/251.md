# Session 251: Nether Portal Frame Break & Water/Explosion Extinguish

Date: 2026-10-13

## Goal
Implement Nether Portal break and extinguishing mechanics (Milestone 133), allowing water contact, frame mining, and explosions to deactivate active portal interior blocks.

## What was done
- `src/world/portal.zig`:
  - Added `isPortalCellValid` validating that a portal block is enclosed in an intact frame.
  - Added `collectPortalCellsToExtinguish` scanning local volume for connected portal blocks.
  - Unit tests verifying frame validity and connected portal cell discovery on broken frames.
- `src/main.zig`:
  - Mining obsidian checks adjacent portal blocks; if frame integrity is breached, extinguishes interior cells to air with particles and sizzle audio.
  - Water contact directly washes/quenches portal blocks within range.
  - Explosions (TNT and overcharge craters) that destroy obsidian or portal blocks extinguish affected portal cells.
- Verification: clean build, all 1444 unit tests passing.
