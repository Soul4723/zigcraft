# Session 334: Milestone 216 — Kelp Plants, Dried Kelp Food, Dried Kelp Fuel Blocks & Oceanic Forest Generation

## Summary
Implemented the aquatic Kelp plant pipeline (`BlockId.kelp = 125`, `BlockId.dried_kelp_block = 126`, `ItemType.kelp = 187`, `ItemType.dried_kelp = 188`, `ItemType.dried_kelp_block = 189`), procedural ocean kelp forest stalk generation in `TerrainGenerator`, ladder-swimming physics through kelp fronds, smelting of Kelp into Dried Kelp in furnaces and smokers, rapid eating mechanics for Dried Kelp restoring hunger, Dried Kelp Block compact storage crafting (Recipe 136) and unpacking (Recipe 137), and dense fuel combustion feeding furnaces.
