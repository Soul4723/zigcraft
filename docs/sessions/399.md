# Session 399: Milestone 480 - Totem of Undying Resurrection Mechanics & Visual Animation

## Goal
Implement Milestone 480: Totem of Undying Resurrection Mechanics & Visual Animation in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/totem_undying_resurrection.zig`.
- **Totem of Undying Activation Trigger**:
  - Slot check & Hand Priority: Evaluates lethal damage (HP <= 0.0); checks offhand slot FIRST, then main hand slot.
  - Consumes exactly 1 Totem of Undying item (`is_consumed = true`) from the holding slot.
  - Supports official ID 449 (`ItemType.totem_of_undying`) and legacy ID 330 (`ItemType.totem_legacy`).
  - Totems held in general inventory slots without being equipped in either hand do not trigger.
- **Death Cancellation & Health Restoration**:
  - Death event cancelled: Player does NOT die, inventory is NOT dropped, and experience points are NOT lost (`death_cancelled = true`, `player_died = false`).
  - Immediate HP reset: Sets player health to **1.0 HP** (half heart).
  - Status Cleansing: Immediately removes all negative status effects: Poison, Fatal Poison, Wither, Slowness, Weakness, Mining Fatigue, Levitation, Darkness, Bad Omen.
  - Positive status effects (e.g. Speed, Strength, Haste) are preserved across resurrection.
- **Resurrection Buff Suite**:
  - **Regeneration II** for 45.0 seconds (900 ticks): Restores 0.8 HP/s (0.04 HP/tick, total +36.0 HP recovery), clamped to max health.
  - **Absorption II** for 5.0 seconds (100 ticks): Adds 4 extra golden absorption hearts (+8.0 absorption HP) that absorb incoming damage before regular HP and expire after 100 ticks.
  - **Fire Resistance I** for 40.0 seconds (800 ticks): Completely prevents fire/lava damage, preserving any existing longer fire resistance timer.
- **Visual & Audio Resurrection Feedback**:
  - Floating Totem Animation: Screen-filling floating golden wings and emerald eyes totem asset overlay (`floating_totem_active = true`, 30 ticks / 1.5s).
  - Emits 32 golden and emerald sparkle ring particles (`TotemParticle`: yellow/green RGBA bursts, radial velocity, upward drift, drag decay).
  - Plays majestic Totem activation chime sound event (`SoundEffect.totem_use`).
- **Void Death Exemption**:
  - Void damage (Y < -64.0 in the Void) and `/kill` commands (`DamageType.command_kill`) cannot be prevented by Totem of Undying; player dies regardless and totem is preserved unconsumed.
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/totem_undying_resurrection.zig` in `build.zig`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Implemented 45 unit tests covering:
    - Offhand and main hand triggers
    - Offhand priority over main hand
    - Inventory check (unheld totems do not save player)
    - Stack decrement (stack of 1 becomes none; stack of 2 becomes 1)
    - 1.0 HP health reset
    - Death cancellation, inventory retention, and experience retention
    - Cleansing of all 9 negative status effects individually and simultaneously
    - Positive effect preservation
    - Regeneration II duration and +36 HP healing rate
    - Absorption II +8 HP hearts, damage absorption, and 100-tick expiration
    - Fire Resistance I duration, fire/lava damage immunity, and duration preservation
    - Void damage exemption (DamageType.void, /kill command, Y < -64 void fall)
    - Void boundary edge cases (Y = -64.0 vs Y = -63.99)
    - Zero damage and non-lethal damage unconsumed checks
    - Floating totem 30-tick animation state and transform curves
    - 32-particle golden and emerald sparkle ring emission and kinematics
    - Totem chime sound event generation
    - Chained consecutive resurrection with multiple totems
