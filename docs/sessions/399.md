# Session 397: Milestone 479 - Evoker Vex Summoning & Fangs Attack Kinematics

## Goal
Implement Milestone 479: Evoker Vex Summoning & Fangs Attack Kinematics in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/evoker_fangs_vex.zig`.
- **Evoker Entity Attributes & Spellcasting State Machine**:
  - Implemented `EvokerEntity` with max health of 24.0 HP (12 hearts), tracking current spell, warmup duration, arm-raising animation posture, and cooldowns.
  - Implemented `SpellType` enum:
    - `SpellType.none`: idle / wandering / fleeing posture with arms lowered.
    - `SpellType.fangs`: arm-raising casting animation (40 ticks / 2.0s) with dark purple particle swirls (`ParticleType.spell_mob` with RGBA `[4]f32{0.38, 0.15, 0.58, 1.0}`).
    - `SpellType.vex`: arm-raising casting animation (100 ticks / 5.0s) with white particle swirls (`[4]f32{1.0, 1.0, 1.0, 1.0}`).
    - `SpellType.wololo`: converts blue sheep to red sheep within 16.0m (60 ticks / 3.0s arm-raising animation with orange-yellow particle swirls `[4]f32{0.85, 0.65, 0.15, 1.0}`).
  - Cooldown management: fangs cooldown (100 ticks / 5.0s), vex cooldown (340 ticks / 17.0s), wololo cooldown (120 ticks / 6.0s), global cooldown (20 ticks / 1.0s).
  - Tactical AI & Fleeing: flees away from threats within 3.0m at 3.0 m/s when appropriate; spell selection prioritizes close defensive circle fangs (within 3.0m), Vex summoning if below active cap of 8, offensive line fangs at range, and wololo conversion on blue sheep outside combat.
- **Evoker Fangs Attack Kinematics & Lifecycle**:
  - Formations:
    - **Offensive Line**: row of 16 fangs extending in a straight line toward target up to 20 blocks away, with 1.25m spacing between fangs and 1-tick sequential activation delay.
    - **Defensive Circle**: two concentric rings surrounding the Evoker (8 inner fangs at radius 1.5m, 12 outer fangs at radius 3.0m, totaling 20 fangs) triggered when target is within 3.0m.
  - Lifecycle:
    - Warning phase: teeth emerge slightly from ground for 14 ticks (0.7s).
    - Snap phase: jaws snap shut with mechanical snap sound (`SoundEffect.fang_snap`), dealing 6.0 HP unmitigated magic damage.
    - Bypasses armor points and shield blocks completely (0% armor absorption, shield block bypassed with zero shield durability damage).
    - Retraction phase: disappears after 20 ticks (1.0s) of retraction, despawning cleanly.
    - Friendly fire immunity: fangs do not damage the summoning Evoker or allied illagers.
- **Vex Entity Summoning & Flight Dynamics**:
  - Summons 2 to 4 Vex entities (`VexEntity`) with `summonVexCount` and `summonVexSquadron`.
  - Max health: 14.0 HP (7 hearts).
  - Weapon: Iron Sword dealing 9.0 HP damage on Normal difficulty, 13.0 HP on Hard difficulty, and 5.0 HP on Easy difficulty.
  - Flying Phasing: ignores solid block collision (`canPhaseThroughBlocks() == true`), phasing smoothly through solid walls, floors, and ceilings.
  - Charge attack: accelerates toward target at 8.0 m/s with red glow (`[3]u8{255, 0, 0}`) for 40 ticks before reverting to cruise speed (3.0 m/s).
  - Life decay (starvation): decay delay clamped between 30 and 119 seconds (600–2380 ticks); begins taking 1.0 HP/s damage (0.05 HP/tick) after delay expires or immediately upon summoner death, dying naturally when health reaches 0.
- **Wololo Sheep Color Conversion**:
  - `wololoConvertSheep`: converts sheep with `SheepColor.blue` within 16.0m to `SheepColor.red` with sound cue, ignoring sheep beyond 16.0m or non-blue colors.
- **Build Registration & Testing**:
  - Registered `src/world/evoker_fangs_vex.zig` in `build.zig`.
  - Implemented 55 comprehensive unit tests covering all attributes, casting states, particle swirls, AI selection, formations, lifecycle phases, damage bypass, vex flight dynamics, phasing, charge attack, life decay, and wololo conversion.
