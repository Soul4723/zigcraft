    - Slot toggling API (`toggleSlot`, `setDisabled`, `isDisabled`, `disabledCount`, `enabledCount`).
    - Disabling an occupied slot ejects/clears its contents, and disabled slots refuse direct player or hopper insertion.
  - **Redstone Rising-Edge Execution & 4-Tick Delay**:
    - Triggers crafting on redstone pulse rising edge. Steady/held high signal does not repeatedly trigger.
    - Requires all enabled slots to contain matching recipe ingredients.
    - Consumes 1 item per active ingredient slot upon successful craft.
    - Ejection from configured front nozzle (`NozzleOutput` with 6-direction vector math).
    - Minimum 4-tick craft delay between consecutive craft operations (`craft_delay_ticks = 4`).
  - **Hopper Round-Robin Sequential Distribution**:
    - `HopperRoundRobin` begins insertion at the lowest enabled slot, advancing sequentially across enabled slots.
    - Skips disabled slots and full slots; wraps from slot 8 back to slot 0.
    - Handles backpressure (`backpressured: bool`) when all enabled slots are full or when all 9 slots are disabled.
  - **Redstone Comparator Fullness Math**:
    - Signal formula: $\text{signal} = \min(9, \text{count}(\text{disabled}) + \text{count}(\text{occupied}))$ ($0..9$ as `u4`).
  - **Unit Testing & Verification**:
