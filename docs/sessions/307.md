# Session 307: Milestone 189 — Creeper Mob Variant, Gunpowder Drops, Primed Fuse Hissing & Blast Detonation Mechanics

## Summary
Implemented the iconic Creeper mob variant (`MobType.creeper`), Gunpowder item (`ItemType.gunpowder = 146`), Recipe 107 (Gunpowder + Sand -> TNT), synthesized `.mob_hiss` acoustic fuse sound, 6-box quadruped creeper mesh geometry, proximity fuse priming (within 3m), escape abort (past 6m), ticking white swell VFX, concussive crater carving detonation, and player launch impulses.

## Changes
- **Audio Synthesizer (`src/audio/synth.zig`)**:
  - Added `.mob_hiss` to `SoundType` enum (~1500ms duration).
  - Synthesized dynamic white noise hiss with rising frequency modulation and amplitude envelope.
  - Unit test: `creeper mob_hiss audio synthesis`.
- **Item & Crafting (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.gunpowder = 146` (HUD tint `(0.38, 0.40, 0.42, 0.95)`).
  - Added `MobType.creeper` with gunpowder mob meat drop via `getMobMeatDrop(.creeper) = .gunpowder`.
  - Recipe 107: 1 Sand + 1 Gunpowder -> 1 TNT (and 2 Sand + 2 Gunpowder checkerboard).
  - Unit tests: verified gunpowder stack size, recipe execution, and mob drop.
- **Mob Attributes & Renderer (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Configured creeper attributes: 10 HP, 0.6m width, 1.7m height, 2.5 m/s speed, `.mob_hiss` sound.
  - Added `fuse_timer: f32 = 0.0` and `is_primed: bool = false` state fields to `Mob`.
  - Built 6-box creeper mesh geometry: mottled green torso (0.40x0.64x0.24), frowning face skull (0.36 cube), and four quadruped legs (0.14x0.50x0.14) with walking gait swing (144 verts, 216 inds).
  - Unit tests: verified creeper stats, sound mapping, and mesh vertex/index counts.
- **Explosion & AI Mechanics (`src/world/explosives.zig`, `src/entity/mob_manager.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `creeper_fuse_time = 1.5`, `creeper_blast_radius = 3`, `creeper_prime_radius = 3.0`, and `creeper_abort_radius = 6.0` with distance threshold check functions.
  - Cave and night spawner tables updated to spawn creepers (`roll_m < 0.25 => .creeper`).
  - Creeper AI: when player is within 3m, creeper primes fuse and plays 3D spatial `.mob_hiss` audio. While ticking down (1.5s), emits white debris and smoke particles. If player retreats beyond 6m, fuse aborts.
  - Detonation carves blast sphere crater, chains adjacent TNT blocks, applies armor-mitigated damage and launch impulse to the player, inflicts blast damage on adjacent mobs, and removes the exploded creeper.
