# Session 312: Milestone 194 — The End Dimension Procedural Island Generation, Obsidian Pillars & Transit Warp

## Summary
Implemented The End dimension procedural terrain generator (`EndGenerator`), central End stone island generation with 4 colossal obsidian monolith pillars, `EndPortalTransitState` rapid dimension shifting mechanics, purple void sky dome, and ambient void particles.

## Changes
- **End Dimension Generation (`src/world/terrain.zig`)**:
  - Added `EndGenerator` with seed initialization.
  - `isIsland` & `islandFloorCeil`: generates circular central End Stone island of radius 40 around (0, 0) with thickness taper from center (y=4..12) to perimeter.
  - `isObsidianPillar`: generates 4 colossal 3x3 square obsidian monolith columns rising from island floor up to world ceiling (y=15) at (16, 16), (-16, 16), (16, -16), and (-16, -16).
  - `generateChunk`: generates chunks when `manager.is_end` is true, populating end stone and obsidian pillars in a starry void.
  - Unit tests: verified island radius, taper elevation, monolith placement, and chunk block placement.
- **Chunk Manager Integration (`src/chunk_manager.zig`)**:
  - Added `end: EndGenerator` and `is_end: bool = false` state.
  - Chunk streaming generates via `self.end.generateChunk` when `self.is_end` is active.
- **Dimension Transit Mechanics (`src/world/portal.zig`)**:
  - Extended `Dimension` enum with `the_end = 2`.
  - Added `EndPortalTransitState` handling rapid dimension transition (0.25s inside portal block) with cooldown immunity.
  - Unit tests: verified state machine and dimension toggling.
- **Atmosphere & Main Loop (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Player standing in `end_portal` triggers `end_portal_transit`.
  - Warping to The End spawns player onto the obsidian platform at (0.5, 11.2, 0.5), triggers `.portal_warp` and `.mob_teleport` audio, burst particles, and violet toast.
  - Stepping back into the portal returns player safely to their Overworld spawn point.
  - Render loop integrates deep purple void sky dome (`end_sky_top = (0.02, 0.01, 0.04)`, `end_sky_horizon = (0.08, 0.03, 0.12)`), dark void clear color, and drifting ambient portal shimmer particles.
