# Session 338: Milestone 220 — Rails, Powered Rails, Minecart Vehicle & Rail Transit System

## Summary
Implemented the Rail transport pipeline: Standard Rails (`BlockId.rail = 128`, `ItemType.rail = 196`), Powered Rails (`BlockId.powered_rail = 129`, `ItemType.powered_rail = 197`), and Minecarts (`ItemType.minecart = 198`, stack 1 vehicle). Added crafting recipes 139, 140, 141, minecart boarding/dismounting mechanics, and accelerated rail cruising physics.

## Changes
- **Blocks & World Properties (`src/world/block.zig`)**:
  - Added `BlockId.rail = 128` and `BlockId.powered_rail = 129`.
  - Non-solid, transparent, hardness 0.7, stone footstep.
  - Powered rail emissive glow 0.6.
  - Texture mapping: rail steel/wood tracks `(1, 2)`, powered rail redstone-infused gold tracks `(7, 7)`.
  - Helper predicate: `BlockId.is_rail()`.
- **Items & Crafting Recipes (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.rail = 196`, `ItemType.powered_rail = 197`, `ItemType.minecart = 198` (stack 1).
  - Roundtrip block mapping (`toBlockId()` / `fromBlockId()`).
  - Recipe 139: Rail — 2 Iron Ingots in vertical column + 1 Stick -> 4 Rails.
  - Recipe 140: Powered Rail — 2 Gold Nuggets + 1 Redstone Dust + 1 Stick -> 4 Powered Rails.
  - Recipe 141: Minecart — 3 Iron Ingots in U-shape -> 1 Minecart.
- **Player Kinematics & Rail Transit (`src/player.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `Player.in_minecart: bool`, `Player.on_rail: bool`, `Player.on_powered_rail: bool`.
  - Rail cruising speed scale: `minecart_rail_speed_scale = 2.0` (2x standard walking speed).
  - Powered rail acceleration speed scale: `minecart_powered_rail_speed_scale = 3.5` (3.5x boost).
  - Right-click rail track with held minecart boards the minecart directly.
  - Left Shift dismounts minecart and safely recovers the vehicle into inventory.
  - Track friction sparks & metallic wheel clatter VFX while riding.
