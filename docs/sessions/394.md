# Session 394: Milestone 474 - Shulker Bullet Guided Tracking & Levitation Kinematics

## Goal
Implement Milestone 474: Shulker Bullet Guided Tracking & Levitation Kinematics in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/shulker_bullet_levitation.zig`.
- **Shulker Bullet Missile Entity**:
  - Implemented `ShulkerBullet` with position (`[3]f32`), velocity (`[3]f32`), `target_entity_id`, target position, and lifetime tracking.
  - Cruise speed configured at 0.50 m/s (`CRUISE_SPEED = 0.50`, `0.025` blocks/tick at 20 TPS).
  - Cardinal axis guided navigation: the missile navigates in orthogonal steps along the 3 primary axes (X, Y, Z) rather than direct diagonal flight, re-evaluating target offset direction every 4 game ticks. Exactly one velocity component is non-zero during flight.
  - Emits spinning white spiral swirling particles (`ParticleType.end_rod`) rotating with tick-based angular advance around the bullet's travel direction.
- **Impact & Shield Deflection Physics**:
  - Direct hit on unshielded entity:
    - Deals 4.0 HP damage (2 hearts).
    - Inflicts **Levitation I** status effect for **10.0 seconds** (200 game ticks).
    - Bullet is destroyed upon impact.
  - Shield blocking:
    - Player blocking with a Shield facing the bullet's incoming vector absorbs 100% of damage (0 HP dealt) and completely negates Levitation.
    - Emits shield block metallic clang sound (`SoundEffect.shield_block_metallic_clang`).
    - Inflicts shield durability wear.
  - Attack Deflection:
    - Striking the incoming Shulker Bullet with any melee attack (fist, sword, axe) or projectile (arrow) destroys the bullet or deflects it away (`is_deflected = true`, deals 0 damage to player).
- **Levitation Status Effect Kinematics**:
  - `LevitationEffect`: Level I applies constant upward vertical velocity `vy = +0.05 * level blocks/tick` = +1.0 m/s (Level II: +2.0 m/s).
  - Overrides normal downward gravity: entity floats smoothly upward without downward acceleration.
  - Horizontal movement remains player-controllable and subject to horizontal air drag (0.91).
  - Drinking a milk bucket immediately cancels Levitation and restores normal downward gravity acceleration on the subsequent tick.
  - Expiration after 200 ticks (10.0 seconds) immediately restores normal downward gravity.
- **Build Registration & Testing**:
  - Registered `src/world/shulker_bullet_levitation.zig` in `build.zig`.
  - Added 42 comprehensive unit tests covering cardinal navigation, orthogonal velocity, 4-tick re-evaluation, particle spiral emission, unshielded damage, shield blocking & sound, attack deflection, levitation upward velocity & gravity override, milk bucket curing, multi-tick trajectory simulation, and 60-second despawn timeout.
