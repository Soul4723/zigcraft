# Session 419: Airborne End Ship Generation & Elytra Vault

Date: 2026-10-29

Implemented `src/world/end_ship_vault.zig` as a deterministic standalone
structure model. The ship bounds are X=80..88, Y=80..95, Z=-2..2 relative to
an End City origin at (0, 64, 0). The model includes a curved purpur keel,
deck and railings, central purpur mast with End Rod yardarms/rigging, a forward
dragon head with 0.25 emissive eyes, a lower-deck Brewing Stand holding two
Instant Health II potions, two loot chests with enchanted diamond gear and
iron/gold/emeralds, the Elytra item frame, and a Shulker attached to the vault
ceiling.

Registered the module in `build.zig` and added its deterministic generator query
to the main loop. The world owner still needs to apply generated blocks, loot
and potion container data, and the Shulker entity at runtime.
