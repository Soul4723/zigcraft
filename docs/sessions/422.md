## What was done

- Implemented `src/world/mace_velocity_scaling.zig` featuring:
  - **Height Falling Kinetic Damage Scaling**:
    - Activation threshold: `SMASH_MIN_FALL_DISTANCE = 1.5` meters. Falls below 1.5m execute standard weapon attacks with base damage 6.0.
    - Two-tier kinetic damage formula:
      - First tier ($0 \le h \le 3.0\text{m}$): adds $+4.0$ damage per meter fallen ($4.0 \times \min(h, 3.0)$).
      - Second tier ($h > 3.0\text{m}$): adds $+2.0$ damage per meter fallen ($2.0 \times \max(0.0, h - 3.0)$).
    - Density enchantment (Levels I–V): adds $+0.5 \times \text{level} \times h$ bonus damage per meter fallen (up to $+2.5\text{ dmg/m}$ at Density V).
    - Breach enchantment (Levels I–IV): reduces target's effective armor points by $15\% \times \text{level}$ (up to 60% armor defense reduction at Breach IV), bounded at 0 armor points.
    - Wind Burst enchantment (Levels I–III): triggers vertical launch impulses of $+7.0\text{ m/s}$ (I), $+10.0\text{ m/s}$ (II), and $+13.0\text{ m/s}$ (III) upon successful smash attack landing.
  - **Aerial Combo Multiplier Progression**:
    - Successive aerial smash landings without touching ground increment `combo_count` ($1 \to 2 \to 3 \dots$).
    - Damage multiplier formula:
      `multiplier = 1.0 + 0.25 * (combo_count - 1)`
      (e.g., Combo 1: $1.0\times$, Combo 2: $1.25\times$, Combo 3: $1.50\times$, Combo 4: $1.75\times$, Combo 5: $2.00\times$, capped at $2.0\times$).
    - Ground contact (`onGroundContact`) resets combo count to 0.
  - **Fall Damage Cancellation**:
    - Landing a successful smash attack completely negates all accumulated fall damage and resets fall distance to 0, even from 100m or 1,000m drops.
  - **Unit Testing & Verification**:
    - 50 unit tests covering activation thresholds, two-tier formulas, Density I–V, Breach I–IV, Wind Burst I–III, aerial combo chains, ground contact resets, and extreme fall damage cancellation.
    - Registered `src/world/mace_velocity_scaling.zig` in `build.zig`.
