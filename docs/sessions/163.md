## Goal

Phase 8 / Milestone 45: Underwater visual effects (fog, color tinting, and atmosphere). Add underwater blue atmosphere, depth fog, and sky/clear background tinting when the player's camera is submerged in water.

## What was done

- `src/renderer/shader.zig`:
  - Updated `chunk_frag` GLSL fragment shader with uniforms `u_underwater` (float) and `u_cam_pos` (vec3).
  - Implemented underwater blue tinting (`vec3(0.5, 0.75, 1.1)`) and distance-based depth fog fading to murky aquatic blue (`vec3(0.08, 0.28, 0.58)`).
  - Added pure helper function `calculateUnderwaterFog(dist: f32) f32` with unit tests for distance ramping.
- `src/chunk_renderer.zig`:
  - Extended `ChunkRenderer.draw` signature to accept `cam_pos: math.Vec3` and `is_underwater: bool`.
  - Bound uniforms `u_underwater` and `u_cam_pos`.
- `src/chunk_manager.zig`:
  - Passed `cam_pos` and `is_underwater` through `ChunkManager.draw` into `ChunkRenderer.draw`.
- `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig` & `src/entity/mob_manager.zig`:
  - Extended `MobRenderer.drawMobs` and `MobManager.draw` to pass `cam_pos` and `is_underwater` to ensure mobs receive identical underwater fog & tinting.
- `src/main.zig`:
  - Sampled block at camera position (`cam.position`).
  - Switched `glClearColor` to aquatic blue (`0.08, 0.28, 0.58`) when submerged.
  - Drew sky dome with deep water gradient colors instead of celestial sky when underwater.
  - Passed camera underwater flag and camera position to chunk and mob rendering passes.
