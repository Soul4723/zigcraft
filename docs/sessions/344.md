# Session 344: Milestone 226 — Slime Mob Variant, Slimeball Drops, Slime Block & Fall Damage Bounce Kinematics

## Summary
Implemented the Slime mob variant, Slimeball drops, craftable Slime Blocks, and vertical rebound bounce kinematics:
- **Slime Mob Entity**: `MobType.slime` (16 HP, 0.8x0.8m, 2.0 m/s speed, drops `ItemType.slimeball`).
- **Slime Visual Geometry & Locomotion**: 4-box articulated green translucent gelatinous mesh (outer body, inner dense nucleus core, 2 dark green eyes); periodic forward bouncy leaps on ground contact (`vel.y = 4.8`).
- **Items & Crafting**:
  - `ItemType.slimeball = 206` (stack size 64).
  - `ItemType.slime_block = 207` / `BlockId.slime_block = 134` (hardness 0.0, transparent, grass footstep, green cube tile `(2, 7)`).
  - Recipe 149: 4 Slimeballs -> 1 Slime Block.
  - Recipe 150: 1 Slime Block -> 4 Slimeballs.
- **Slime Block Bounce Mechanics**:
  - `player.tickPhysicsCustom` with `slime` block probe callback (`pickSlime`).
  - Landing on a solid slime block nullifies fall damage completely and converts impact downward speed into upward rebound velocity at 80% elasticity (`vel_y = min(20.0, -fall_speed * 0.80)`).
- **Spawning & Hostility**:
  - Integrated into deep underground cave mob spawn tables.
  - Integrated into hostile mob AI tracking and melee contact combat.
