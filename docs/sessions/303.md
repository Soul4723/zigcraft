# Session 303: Milestone 185 - Spider Eye Item, Potion of Poison Brewing & Toxic Affliction Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the Spider Eye item (`ItemType.spider_eye = 140`), Potion of Poison (`ItemType.poison_potion = 141`, Recipe 103), brewing stand toxic distillation pipeline, poison affliction vital mechanics (`poison_timer`, 1 HP non-lethal floor, healing suppression), and spider melee envenomation.

## Changes
- **Item Definitions & Crafting (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.spider_eye = 140` (stack 64, HUD tint `0.65, 0.12, 0.18`).
  - Added `ItemType.poison_potion = 141` (HUD tint `0.35, 0.68, 0.22`).
  - Added deterministic 33% spider drop algorithm `dropsSpiderEye(wx, wy, wz)`.
  - Implemented Recipe 103: 1 Spider Eye + 1 Glass vial (or 1 Nether Wart) -> 1 Potion of Poison.
  - Unit tests: `spider eye item properties and drop probability` and `poison potion recipe and item properties`.
- **Player Vital Mechanics (`src/player.zig`)**:
  - Added `poison_timer: f32 = 0.0`.
  - Added `applyPoison()` (10.0s poison duration).
  - Implemented authentic poison drain behavior: damages health at 1.0 HP/s but clamps at a non-lethal floor of 1.0 HP (cannot kill, unlike Wither effect). Suppresses healing while active.
  - Unit test: `player poison effect drains health down to 1 HP floor`.
- **Brewing Pipeline & Gameplay Integration (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Expanded `BrewingStandState` with `spider_eyes` and `poison_potions` inventory counters.
  - Right-click brewing stand loads spider eyes as brewing reagents and retrieves finished poison potions.
  - Autonomous distillation converts 1 blaze powder + 1 spider eye + 1 glass bottle into 1 Potion of Poison in 2.5s.
  - Drinking poison potion or ingesting raw spider eye inflicts poison effect on player.
  - Spider mob melee bites now envenom player with poison on unblocked attack.
  - Spider defeats (melee and ranged) roll `dropsSpiderEye` to drop spider eyes.
