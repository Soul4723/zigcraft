# Session 316: Milestone 198 — Dragon Egg Trophy Block, Exit Altar Generation & Evasive Teleportation Mechanics

## Summary
Implemented the Dragon Egg trophy block and item (`BlockId.dragon_egg = 109`, `ItemType.dragon_egg = 152`), gravity falling physics, mystical 0.35 violet emissive glow, exit fountain altar generation upon Ender Dragon defeat, and evasive strike teleportation.

## Changes
- **Block Definitions (`src/world/block.zig`)**:
  - Added `dragon_egg = 109` (hardness 3.0, solid, transparent cutouts, gravity falling `is_falling() == true`, emissive 0.35, tile 5, 2 obsidian crystalline shell).
  - Footstep type: `.stone`.
  - Unit tests: verified solidity, transparency, falling gravity, hardness, 0.35 emissive luminescence, and texture mappings.
- **Item & Harvest Drops (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.dragon_egg = 152` (stack 64, HUD tint `0.22, 0.10, 0.28, 0.98`).
  - Item roundtrip mappings (`toBlockId` and `fromBlockId`).
  - Harvest drop mapping: drops 1 `dragon_egg` item when harvested with a pickaxe.
  - Unit tests: verified roundtrips, name, and single-item drop count.
- **Exit Altar & Dragon Egg Spawning (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Ender Dragon boss defeat generates an obsidian center exit fountain at `(0, 8, 0)`:
    - 2-high central obsidian pedestal `(0, 8..9, 0)` topped with the Dragon Egg trophy at `(0, 10, 0)`.
    - Horizontal return `end_portal` cells surrounding the pedestal base.
- **Evasive Strike Teleportation (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Striking the Dragon Egg block triggers `.mob_teleport` audio and 24 portal particles, teleporting the egg to a nearby coordinate within a 5-block radius.
