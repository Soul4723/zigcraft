Branch: `feat/sand-glass-blocks-and-beaches`

## Goal

Add Sand blocks, realistic coastline beach generation around water bodies, and populate the final empty atlas slot (3, 3) with handcrafted golden sand texture.

## What was done

- `src/renderer/texture.zig`:
  - Handcrafted procedural pixel-art Sand texture for atlas tile (3, 3).
  - Uses golden-yellow and warm tan speckled granules mimicking Minecraft beach sand.
  - All 13 block tiles on the 4x4 atlas now fully textured and opaque.
- `src/world/block.zig`:
  - Added `BlockId.sand = 12`.
  - Mapped texture coordinates to atlas tile (3, 3).
  - Hardness 0.5s (breakable quickly by hand or shovel).
- `src/inventory.zig`:
  - Added `ItemType.sand = 14`, mappings `toBlockId` and `fromBlockId`.
  - Name "Sand", particle / hotbar color `(0.91, 0.84, 0.63, 0.95)`.
- `src/world/terrain.zig`:
  - Beaches: columns with surface height at `SEA_LEVEL` or `SEA_LEVEL + 1` generate sand top and sub-layers instead of grass and dirt.
  - Inland forest line: trees now only spawn above beach lines (`h > SEA_LEVEL + 1`), keeping shores clear and realistic.
  - Added unit test `beaches spawn sand near sea level`.
- `src/main.zig`:
  - Added footstep sound mapping for `BlockId.sand` -> `.step_dirt`.
