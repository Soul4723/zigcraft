  - Added `DropperState` tracking placed droppers, redstone activation state, and 4 internal item slots (initialized with cobblestone).

  - Implemented dropper redstone triggering:

    - Otherwise, item drops into world (picked up into player inventory if nearby).
    - Plays click sound and spawns puff debris; plays `.click_off` when empty.
