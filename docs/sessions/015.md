# Session 015

Date: 2026-10-02
Branch: `feat/water-system`

## Goal

Implement Milestone 24: Water fluid blocks, translucent rendering, and sea-level terrain lakes.

## What was done

- **Block & Fluid Properties (`src/world/block.zig`):**
  - Added `BlockId.water = 4`.
  - Configured `is_solid()` to return `false` for water (player can move through it).
  - Configured `is_transparent()` and `is_fluid()` for water.
  - Assigned atlas coordinate `(0, 1)` for water texture.
- **Texture Atlas (`src/renderer/texture.zig`):**
  - Added water tile on row 1 `(0, 1)` with vibrant blue noise and 72% opacity (`alpha = 185`).
  - Added unit test checking UV bounds for water.
- **Chunk Meshing (`src/world/chunk_mesh.zig`):**
  - Added `shouldRenderFace(my_block, chunk, neighbors, nx, ny, nz)`:
    - Water internal boundary culling: water faces adjacent to water are culled to prevent interior quad overdraw.
    - Solid blocks against water render outer faces.
    - Water against air or solid renders.
- **Chunk Renderer (`src/chunk_renderer.zig`):**
  - Enabled alpha blending during chunk draw calls (`GL_BLEND`, `GL_SRC_ALPHA`, `GL_ONE_MINUS_SRC_ALPHA`).
- **World Generation (`src/world/terrain.zig`):**
  - Added `SEA_LEVEL = 5`.
  - Terrain valleys/depressions below sea level are automatically flooded with water blocks up to `SEA_LEVEL`.
- **UI & Hotbar (`src/renderer/ui.zig`, `src/main.zig`):**
  - Added 4th hotbar slot for Water (blue slot indicator).
  - Key `4` selects water for placement.
  - Player can place water or destroy water blocks with mouse clicks.
- **Tests & Verification:**
  - All 20 test suites pass.
  - Live runtime verified: natural water lakes appear in terrain depressions, alpha blending active, 140+ FPS.
