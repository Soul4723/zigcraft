## What was done

- Implemented `src/world/resin_brick_masonry.zig` providing complete resin masonry
  domain logic:
  1. **Smelting Engine & Pyrometallurgy**:
     - Standard furnace: 200 ticks (10.0s) per clump.
     - Blast furnace: 100 ticks (5.0s, 2x speedup) per clump.
     - Incompatible workstation rejection: food smokers and campfires reject resin clumps.
     - Experience yield: 0.1 XP per clump smelted (1.0 XP per 10 clumps).
     - Step-by-step `SmeltingSimulation` with partial tick accumulation, multiple-item
       completion, and banked XP claiming.
  2. **Resin Brick Block Crafting (2x2)**:
     - 4 Resin Bricks in 2x2 grid produce 1 Block of Resin Bricks (`BlockId.resin_bricks`).
     - Hardness 1.5, blast resistance 6.0.
     - Tool requirement: Wooden pickaxe or higher required to drop itself;
       bare hands and non-pickaxes drop 0 items.
     - Fire resistance: Immune to fire spread and lava ignition.
  3. **Architectural Masonry Variations**:
     - **Resin Brick Stairs**: 6 blocks -> 4 stairs; straight, inner left/right,
       and outer left/right corner shape states based on neighbor stair connections;
       lower half vs upper half (upside down); 1.0m collision height.
     - **Resin Brick Slab**: 3 blocks horizontal -> 6 slabs; bottom half (0.5m),
       top half (0.5m), and placing on matching half merges into double slab
       (1.0m height, drops 2 slabs when mined with pickaxe).
     - **Resin Brick Wall**: 6 blocks -> 6 walls; post-and-side cardinal connections
       to adjacent solid blocks and walls; straight NS/EW post lowering; 1.5m jump-proof bounds.
     - **Chiseled Resin Bricks**: 2 slabs stacked vertically -> 1 chiseled block with
       ceremonial carved medallion face.
  4. **Stonecutter 1:1 Recipes**:
     - 1 Resin Bricks -> 1 Resin Brick Stairs (yield 1)
     - 1 Resin Bricks -> 2 Resin Brick Slabs (yield 2)
     - 1 Resin Bricks -> 1 Resin Brick Wall (yield 1)
     - 1 Resin Bricks -> 1 Chiseled Resin Bricks (yield 1)
- Registered `src/world/resin_brick_masonry.zig` in `build.zig`.
