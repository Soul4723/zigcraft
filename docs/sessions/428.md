## Goal

Implement Milestone 532: Pale Moss Carpet & Pale Hanging Moss Spread Dynamics in Zigcraft (`src/world/pale_moss_spread.zig`).

## What was done

- Implemented `src/world/pale_moss_spread.zig` with full domain mechanics:
  - **Pale Moss Carpet Block**:
    - Height: 0.0625m (1 pixel / 1/16th block), non-solid walk-through.
    - Floor and wall attachment (`CarpetSides` tracking bottom, north, south, east, west faces).
    - Support validation: attaches to solid top faces, breaks/pops if underlying block is removed; attaches to solid side faces of Pale Oak wood/logs.
    - Flammability: combustible, ignites from fire or lava contact.
    - Breaking yields 1 `pale_moss_carpet` item.
  - **Pale Hanging Moss Block**:
    - Downward vertical plant with tip growth (length 1 to 8 blocks).
    - Support rule: ceiling support from solid blocks or leaves (`pale_oak_leaves`), or hanging from pale hanging moss directly above.
    - Tip growth dynamics: bottom-most segment marked with `is_tip = true`; growing downward into air demotes old tip to body and places new tip below; stops at max length 8.
    - Cascade breaking physics: breaking any segment instantly breaks all dependent segments below it, while preserving segments above and promoting the new lowest surviving segment to tip.
    - Shears harvesting: breaking with Shears yields Pale Hanging Moss item (100% chance), consumes 1 shears durability, and emits `shears_snip` audio cue.
    - Bare hand breaking: 0% drop chance (drops 0 items) and 0 shears wear.
  - **Bonemeal Growth Reaction on Pale Moss Block**:
    - Target verification: requires `pale_moss_block` and at least 1 bonemeal item.
    - Consumes 1 bonemeal and emits green/white sparkle bonemeal particles (`bonemeal_sparkle` sound).
    - Radiates spread outwards across solid floor blocks within a 5-block horizontal radius ($dx^2 + dz^2 \le 25.0$).
    - Generates Pale Moss Carpet on eligible solid top faces.
    - Climbs vertically up side faces of Pale Oak wood/logs (up to 2 blocks high above the floor/trunk base).
    - Generates downward hanging Pale Hanging Moss beneath adjacent Pale Oak leaf blocks (1 to 3 blocks length).
  - **3D World Simulation Grid**:
    - Implemented `PaleMossWorld` bounded container for coordinate manipulation, queries, physics updates, and tests.
- Implemented 45 unit tests covering:
  - Carpet 0.0625m height, walk-through non-solid collision, placement, detachment, drops, flammability, fire and lava ignition.
  - Hanging moss non-solid collision, ceiling support (solids and leaves), lower segment support, 1..8 block length limits.
  - Tip growth step-by-step, flag transitions, obstruction blocking, 8-block length ceiling.
  - Cascade break on ceiling removal, middle segment destruction, bottom tip break, shears durability and audio.
  - Bare hand and tool drops vs. shears guarantee.
  - Bonemeal validation, consumption, sparkle particles, 5-block radius bounds, carpet placement, vertical 2-block log climb, canopy hanging moss generation, obstructed leaf checks.
