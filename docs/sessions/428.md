## What was done

- Implemented `src/world/eyeblossom_bloom_toxicity.zig` featuring:
  - **Eyeblossom State Machine & Nocturnal Transition**:
    - `EyeblossomState`: `.closed` vs. `.open`.
    - **Closed Eyeblossom**:
      - Daytime condition: game ticks `0..12,999` or `23,001..24,000`, or skylight `> 7`.
      - Appearance: closed grey petals (`petalColorRgba = .{ 0.55, 0.55, 0.55, 1.0 }`), no eye visible (`eye_visible = false`), non-emissive (`emissive = 0.0`).
    - **Open Eyeblossom**:
      - Nighttime condition: game ticks `13,000..23,000` or skylight `\le 7`.
      - Appearance: petals bloom open revealing glowing orange iris (`irisColorRgba = .{ 1.0, 0.48, 0.0, 1.0 }`), emissive glow (`emissive = 0.6`).
      - Ambient Particle Emission: emits ambient drifting orange eye sparkle particles (`EyeSparkleParticle` with upward drift velocity and lifetime expiration).
    - **Transition Events**:
      - Dusk opening transition (at tick 13,000 or darkness entry): snaps open, plays `SoundEffect.eyeblossom_open`.
      - Dawn closing transition (at tick 23,000 or light return): snaps shut, plays `SoundEffect.eyeblossom_close`.
  - **Bee Pollination & Toxicity Interaction**:
    - **Closed Eyeblossom Pollination**:
      - Bee pollinating closed eyeblossom gathers nectar safely without adverse effects (`is_poisoned = false`, `is_angered = false`, eyes normal, non-erratic flight).
    - **Open Eyeblossom Pollination**:
      - Bee attempting to pollinate an open eyeblossom receives Poison status effect:
        - Deals 1.0 HP magic damage per second (duration 10.0 seconds / 200 ticks, 20 ticks per damage interval).
      - Bee becomes immediately agitated/angered (`is_angered = true`, eyes glow red, flying erratically).
      - Lethal poison: 10.0 HP bee perishes after full 10-second (200-tick) poison duration if uncured.
      - Poison cure helper (`cureBeePoison`) to neutralize toxicity with milk or honey.
  - **Suspicious Stew Crafting Synergy**:
    - Crafting Suspicious Stew with a Closed Eyeblossom:
      - Yields Suspicious Stew providing **Speed I** buff for 10.0 seconds (200 ticks).
    - Crafting Suspicious Stew with an Open Eyeblossom:
      - Yields Suspicious Stew inflicting **Blindness I** debuff for 10.0 seconds (200 ticks).
    - Recipe validation (`canCraftSuspiciousStew` requiring 1 Red Mushroom + 1 Brown Mushroom + 1 Bowl + 1 Eyeblossom) and player consumption simulation (`PlayerStatus`).
  - **Multi-Eyeblossom Patch Simulation**:
    - `EyeblossomPatch` managing synchronized day/night transitions and event logging across clustered flowers.
  - **Unit Testing & Build Integration**:
    - 52 comprehensive unit tests covering all state machine conditions, day/night and light level triggers, transition sound events, ambient particles, bee pollination safety and toxicity, poison damage intervals, erratic flight kinematics, Suspicious Stew recipes and player consumption effects.
    - Registered `src/world/eyeblossom_bloom_toxicity.zig` in `build.zig` test runner.
    - Added representative frame-loop query in `src/main.zig`.
