# Session 315: Milestone 197 — Ender Dragon Boss Health Bar, End Crystal Tether Healing & Defeat Finale

## Summary
Implemented the top-screen purple boss health bar with dragon crest jewel, real-time End Crystal radiant particle tethering with boss HP regeneration, and catastrophic boss defeat finale with dual audio roar and particle eruption.

## Changes
- **HUD Boss Bar (`src/renderer/ui.zig`)**:
  - Added `boss_health_frac: f32` parameter to `drawHUDWithCompass`.
  - Added top-screen 0.50-width boss health bar frame at `y = 0.88`.
  - Renders glowing magenta health fill (`0.85, 0.20, 0.90, 0.95`) scaled to boss HP (0..200).
  - Renders gold dragon crest jewel atop center of health frame.
- **Boss State Query & Health Bar Integration (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - In The End dimension, queries alive Ender Dragon mob health and calculates `boss_health_frac = m.health / 200.0`.
- **End Crystal Radiant Healing Tether (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Scans the 4 monolith pillar apex coordinates `(+-16, 15, +-16)`.
  - For each intact `end_crystal`, generates a dynamic radiant particle beam (vortex swirls + glowstone sparkles) linking crystal to dragon.
  - Periodically pulses regeneration restoring dragon health back toward 200 HP maximum.
- **Boss Defeat Finale (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Defeating the Ender Dragon triggers a victory celebration:
    - Plays simultaneous `.dragon_roar` (1.0) and `.end_portal_open` (1.0) audio.
    - Erupts 64 firework burst particles and 48 wide-radius vortex particles.
    - Displays 5.0s golden victory toast and telemetry logs.
