# Session 332: Milestone 214 — Dolphin Mob Variant, Dolphin's Grace & Fishing Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the Dolphin playful marine mob (`MobType.dolphin`), Dolphin's Grace player buff accelerating swimming speed by 1.6x, 8m dolphin proximity aura in water, Fishing Rod crafting and line casting mechanics (`ItemType.fishing_rod = 186`), Raw Cod (`ItemType.raw_cod = 184`) and Cooked Cod (`ItemType.cooked_cod = 185`) consumable food items, and furnace/smoker fish cooking pipeline.

## Changes
- **Items & Cooking (`src/inventory.zig`, `src/world/smelting.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.raw_cod = 184`, `ItemType.cooked_cod = 185`, and `ItemType.fishing_rod = 186`.
  - Added Recipe 135: 2 Sticks + 2 Strings (or scute/phantom membrane) -> 1 Fishing Rod (stack 1, durability 64, tool wear enabled).
  - Cod food properties: stack cap 64, compostable, right-click eating restores hunger (raw restores 2, cooked restores 5).
  - Smelting and smoking recipes: `RawCod -> CookedCod` (evaluates in both furnace and high-speed smoker).
- **Dolphin Mob Variant (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`, `src/entity/mob_manager.zig`)**:
  - Added `MobType.dolphin` (10 HP, 0.9m width, 0.6m height, 4.8 m/s swim speed in water, 0.4 m/s on land, drops `ItemType.raw_cod` on defeat).
  - 6-box sleek cetacean mesh geometry:
    - Torso fuselage (0.40 x 0.42 x 0.85) in smooth blue-grey skin with pale belly.
    - Beak / snout (0.18 x 0.16 x 0.28).
    - Dorsal fin (0.08 x 0.24 x 0.26).
    - Articulated left & right pectoral flippers with counter-rolling swim animation.
    - Caudal tail fluke (0.45 x 0.08 x 0.25) undulating with pitch wave.
  - Marine spawning table: dolphins spawn naturally in daytime Overworld water pools.
- **Dolphin's Grace Buff (`src/player.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `dolphins_grace_timer: f32 = 0.0` to `Player` with `applyDolphinsGrace()`, `hasDolphinsGrace()`, and vitals decay in `tickVitals`.
  - Water movement physics: boosts player swimming horizontal speed by 1.6x when `hasDolphinsGrace()` is true.
  - Proximity aura: swimming within 8m of a live Dolphin in water grants 30s Dolphin's Grace buff with bubble bursts and toast notification.
- **Fishing Rod Casting Mechanic (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Right-click with held Fishing Rod casts line:
    - If aimed at water within 8m or standing in water, catches 1 Raw Cod into player inventory.
    - Emits splash audio, water splash particles, and bubble burst.
    - Degrades Fishing Rod tool durability via `tool_wear.use` until breakage.
