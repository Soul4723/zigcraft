# Session 401: Milestone 482 - Piglin Brute & Bastion Defense Tactics

## Goal
Implement Milestone 482: Piglin Brute & Bastion Defense Tactics in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/piglin_brute_bastion.zig`.
- **Piglin Brute Entity Attributes**:
  - Max Health: **50.0 HP** (25 hearts), over 3x standard Piglin health (16.0 HP, 3.125x ratio).
  - Base attack damage: **13.0 HP** with Golden Axe heavy blow.
  - Visual Identifiers: Golden arm band (`arm_band_color = .{ 1.0, 0.84, 0.0, 1.0 }`), scarred blind eye on left side, and two-handed Golden Axe combat stance.
  - Non-respawning persistence: Spawns only upon world generation in Bastion Remnants; never respawns when killed (`spawns_only_on_generation = true`, `can_respawn = false`, `is_structure_persistent = true`).
- **Gold Distraction & Pacification Immunity**:
  - Completely immune to gold armor pacification: Attacks players unconditionally on sight regardless of whether they wear any gold armor or a full golden suit (`is_gold_armor_ignored = true`, `is_always_hostile = true`).
  - Completely immune to gold bartering distraction: Never picks up gold ingots, blocks, or nuggets; never barters; never pauses to inspect gold (`is_barter_ignored = true`, `canPickUpItem = false`, `attemptBarter = false`).
- **Bastion Defense Alarm Coordination**:
  - Alarm Sound Event: Emits piercing war snort audio alarm (`SoundEffect.brute_war_horn`) when spotting or attacking a player.
  - Broadcast Radius: Coordinates alarm broadcast across a **16.0m Euclidean radius** (`ALARM_RADIUS = 16.0`).
  - Pacification Break: All standard Piglins within 16.0m Euclidean distance immediately break pacification (`is_pacified = false`), drop any gold ingots they are inspecting (`dropped_ingot_count += 1`), become aggressive (`is_angry = true`), and join the attack against the target player.
  - Piglins outside the 16.0m radius remain pacified / inspecting.
- **Overworld & The End Zombification**:
  - Exposure to Overworld or The End initiates shivering / shaking state (`is_shaking = true`).
  - After 15.0 seconds (**300 ticks**): transforms into a **Zombified Piglin** with 50.0 HP max health, 13.0 attack damage, Golden Axe, undead status, and fire immunity.
- **Defeat Drops**:
  - Golden Axe weapon: 8.5% base drop chance (`GOLDEN_AXE_BASE_DROP_CHANCE = 0.085`), increasing by +1.0% per Looting level (9.5% at Looting I, 10.5% at Looting II, 11.5% at Looting III).
  - High Experience Drop: **20 Experience Points** (`EXPERIENCE_POINTS_DROP = 20`).
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/piglin_brute_bastion.zig` into `test_files` in `build.zig`.

    - Non-respawning persistence and cannot respawn after death
    - Gold armor wearing ignored across all slots (helmet, chestplate, leggings, boots, full set)
    - Gold ingot, nugget, and block pickup immunity
    - Barter attempt immunity
    - War snort audio alarm emission (`SoundEffect.brute_war_horn`)
