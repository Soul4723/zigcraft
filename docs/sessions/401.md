# Session 401: Milestone 481 - Piglin Bartering & Gold Ingot Economy

## Goal
Implement Milestone 481: Piglin Bartering & Gold Ingot Economy in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/piglin_bartering_economy.zig`.
- **Piglin Gold Pacification**:
  - Golden armor pacification check across all 4 armor slots (`golden_helmet`, `golden_chestplate`, `golden_leggings`, `golden_boots`): wearing at least 1 piece pacifies standard Piglins (`is_pacified = true`, `is_hostile = false`, `is_charging = false`).
  - Players without golden armor leave Piglins hostile (`is_hostile = true`, charges with crossbow or golden sword).
  - Unequipping golden armor immediately breaks pacification and makes Piglin hostile.
  - Piglin Brutes ignore golden armor entirely and remain permanently hostile (`is_brute = true`, `is_hostile = true`, `is_charging = true`).
- **Hostility Triggers**:
  - Attacking any Piglin or Piglin Brute triggers nearby Piglin hostility for 600 ticks (30s) within 16 blocks.
  - Opening guarded containers (chest, trapped chest, shulker box, ender chest, barrel) within 16 blocks breaks pacification.
  - Breaking guarded gold blocks (gold block, gilded blackstone, gold ore, nether gold ore, deepslate gold ore, raw gold block) within 16 blocks breaks pacification.
  - Boundary check: 16.0m triggers hostility; >16.0m leaves Piglin pacified.
  - Hostility trigger emits angry snort sound (`SoundEvent.piglin_angry`) and angry villager particles (`ParticleEvent.angry_villager`).
  - Piglin calms down after 600 ticks if player wears golden armor and no further hostility is provoked.
- **Bartering Interaction & 120-Tick (6.0s) Inspection Phase**:
  - Right-clicking Piglin with Gold Ingot (`ItemType.gold_ingot`) or throwing Gold Ingot within 3.5m starts 120 game ticks (6.0s) inspection.
  - During inspection: Piglin stops moving (`velocity = Vec3.zero()`, `is_moving = false`), holds ingot in hand (`holding_gold_ingot = true`), and ignores distractions (`ignores_distractions = true`).
  - Rejection of non-gold items: right-clicking or throwing non-gold items (iron ingot, diamond, dirt, stone, gold nugget, gold block) is rejected and does not start bartering.
  - Hostile Piglins reject bartering interactions.
  - Active inspection prevents initiating concurrent barters.
- **Bartering Loot Dispensing**:
  - At the completion of 120 ticks: consumes the gold ingot (`consumed_gold_ingot = true`), drops 1 bartered loot item at Piglin position with upward pop velocity (`vy = 0.30`).
  - Emits barter completion sound (`SoundEvent.piglin_barter_complete`) and sparkle particles (`ParticleEvent.barter_sparkle`).
  - Implemented complete 15-entry Nether barter loot pool with weights, nominal probabilities, and count ranges:
    - Soul Speed Enchanted Book (1.09%, count 1)
    - Iron Boots with Soul Speed (1.89%, count 1)
    - Potion of Fire Resistance (2.18%, count 1) / Splash Potion (2.18%, count 1)
    - Ender Pearl (4.36%, count 2..4)
    - Crying Obsidian (8.71%, count 1..3)
    - Obsidian (8.71%, count 1)
    - Fire Charge (8.71%, count 1)
    - Nether Quartz (4.36%, count 5..12)
    - String (4.36%, count 3..9)
    - Magma Cream (4.36%, count 1)
    - Leather (8.71%, count 2..4)
    - Soul Sand (8.71%, count 2..8)
    - Gravel (8.71%, count 8..16)
    - Iron Nugget (2.18%, count 10..36)
  - Baby Piglins take the gold ingot and inspect it, but confiscate the gold upon completion without dispensing loot.
- **Bastion World Economy Simulation**:
  - `PiglinBarterEconomy` simulator managing selective anger radius, broadcasted container and mining triggers, and world-wide loot tick updates.
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/piglin_bartering_economy.zig` in `build.zig`.

    - Distance thresholds (16.0m boundary, 3.5m pickup boundary)
    - Gold ingot consumption and 120-tick inspection timer
    - Motionlessness, holding gold, and ignoring distractions during inspection
    - Rejection of non-gold items
