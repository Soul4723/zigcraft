# Session 331: Milestone 213 — Elder Guardian Boss Variant, Mining Fatigue Debuff & Sponge Harvesting Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the Elder Guardian marine boss (`MobType.elder_guardian`), Mining Fatigue vital debuff on Player slowing block mining speed by 70%, Ocean Monument apex boss chamber generation with Wet Sponge block clusters, 7-box pale-ivory boss geometry with articulated spikes and tail fin, boss health bar integration, Sponge and Wet Sponge block and item pipeline (`BlockId.sponge = 123`, `BlockId.wet_sponge = 124`, `ItemType.sponge = 182`, `ItemType.wet_sponge = 183`), spherical water absorption physics transforming dry sponge into wet sponge, and furnace sponge drying.

## Changes
- **Blocks & Items (`src/world/block.zig`, `src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `BlockId.sponge = 123` and `BlockId.wet_sponge = 124` (solid, opaque, hardness 0.6, grass footstep).
  - Added `ItemType.sponge = 182` and `ItemType.wet_sponge = 183` (stack cap 64, non-tool).
  - Face texture mapping: sponge (0, 3 porous golden-yellow), wet sponge (7, 1 dark saturated).
  - Color palette: sponge golden yellow `.{0.85, 0.80, 0.30}`, wet sponge olive-green `.{0.65, 0.68, 0.28}`.
  - Roundtrip conversions `toBlockId` and `fromBlockId` for sponge and wet sponge.
- **Water Absorption Physics (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Placing dry `BlockId.sponge` evaluates a 3-block radius sphere (`sx*sx + sy*sy + sz*sz <= 9`).
  - Clears all adjacent water blocks to air and transforms placed sponge into wet sponge with splash particle effects and audio.
- **Furnace Drying (`src/world/smelting.zig`)**:
  - Added smelting recipe: `WetSponge -> Sponge` (fuel cost 1 coal/charcoal, 10s smelt time in furnace).
- **Elder Guardian Boss Mob (`src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`, `src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `MobType.elder_guardian` (80 HP, 1.4m width, 1.4m height, 1.8 m/s swim speed in water, 0.3 m/s on land, drops `ItemType.sponge` on defeat, sound `.mob_roar`).
  - 7-box pale-ivory cyclopean marine boss geometry:
    - 1.1m massive body with pale yellowish-ivory scales.
    - 0.5m cyclopean pupil eye aperture on front face.
    - 4 massive kinetic defense spikes (dorsal, ventral, lateral left/right) pulsing with retraction animation.
    - 0.7m articulated swimming tail fin undulating with heavy propulsion wave.
- **Mining Fatigue Debuff (`src/player.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `mining_fatigue_timer: f32 = 0.0` to `Player` with `applyMiningFatigue()`, `hasMiningFatigue()`, and vitals decay in `tickVitals`.
  - When afflicted with Mining Fatigue, block mining speed is reduced by 70% (`need /= 0.3`).
  - Ocean Monument proximity curse: entering within 24m of the Ocean Monument awakens the Elder Guardian at the apex vault and afflicts the player with 60s Mining Fatigue with heavy roar audio and toast warning.
  - Elder Guardian laser attack: 6 contact damage + splash audio + refreshes Mining Fatigue affliction.
- **Boss Health Bar (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Top-of-screen boss health bar dynamically displays Elder Guardian health fraction (`health / 80.0`) when present in Overworld.
- **Ocean Monument Apex Vault (`src/world/terrain.zig`)**:
  - Apex vault at (0, 5..6, -16) generates clusters of wet sponge blocks directly above the central Conduit altar.
