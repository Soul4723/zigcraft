# Session 313: Milestone 195 — End Crystal Block Crafting, Monolith Perching & Explosive Detonation Mechanics

## Summary
Implemented the End Crystal block and item (`BlockId.end_crystal = 108`, `ItemType.end_crystal = 151`), Recipe 111 (2 Glass + 1 Eye of Ender + 1 Blaze Powder/Nether Star/Magma Cream -> 1 End Crystal), procedural perching on obsidian monolith pillar apexes in The End, and volatile concussive blast detonation upon destruction.

## Changes
- **Block Definitions (`src/world/block.zig`)**:
  - Added `end_crystal = 108` (hardness 0.0, non-solid, transparent, emissive 0.85, radiant core top 7,6 / obsidian base bottom 5,2 / glass facet sides 6,6).
  - Configured `is_solid`, `is_transparent`, `emissive`, face textures, hardness, and definitions.
  - Unit tests: verified non-solid, transparent, zero hardness, and 0.85 emissive luminescence.
- **Item & Crafting (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.end_crystal = 151` (stack 64, HUD tint `0.85, 0.30, 0.90, 0.95`).
  - Item roundtrip mappings (`toBlockId` and `fromBlockId`).
  - Recipe 111: 2 Glass + 1 Eye of Ender + 1 Blaze Powder/Nether Star/Magma Cream -> 1 End Crystal.
  - Unit tests: verified item roundtrips, names, and Recipe 111 crafting.
- **Monolith Apex Generation (`src/world/terrain.zig`)**:
  - Added `isPillarApex` checking central coordinate of the 3x3 pillar columns at `(16, 16)`, `(-16, 16)`, `(16, -16)`, and `(-16, -16)`.
  - At world ceiling (y=15), monolith apex perches an `end_crystal` block.
- **Combat & Explosive Detonation (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - Breaking or striking an `end_crystal` triggers concussive detonation.
  - Plays `.firework_blast` audio (1.0).
  - Spawns 36 firework burst particles and 24 portal vortex particles.
  - Carves spherical explosive crater in non-obsidian adjacent blocks.
  - Calculates radial blast damage impulse (1.5x damage scale + upward velocity push) on nearby player.
