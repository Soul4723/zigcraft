# Session 396: Milestone 477 - Skeleton Trap Lightning Summoning & Four Horsemen Combat

## Goal
Implement Milestone 477: Skeleton Trap Lightning Summoning & Four Horsemen Combat in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/skeleton_trap_combat.zig`.
- **Skeleton Trap Horse Spawn & Triggering**:
  - Spawn conditions: Natural lightning strike during thunderstorm (0.75% to 7.5% probability scaled by clamped regional difficulty `[0.0, 1.0]`).
  - Trap horse entity: Appears as a peaceful passive Skeleton Horse wandering in thunderstorm.
  - Proximity Trigger: When any player approaches within 10.0 meters Euclidean distance (`TRIGGER_DISTANCE = 10.0`):
    - Triggers cosmetic lightning strike at horse's exact position (without inflicting damage to horse or player).
    - Instantly transforms the single trap horse into 4 mounted Skeleton Horsemen (`TheFourHorsemen`).
- **Four Horsemen Entity Attributes**:
  - 4 Skeleton Riders mounted on 4 Skeleton Horses (`[4]SkeletonHorseman`).
  - Skeleton Riders:
    - Health: 20.0 HP.
    - Equipment: Equipped with Enchanted Iron Helmet (prevents sunlight burn damage with durability wear) and Enchanted Bow (+2.0 power damage).
    - Damage immunity: 3.0 seconds (60 ticks) damage immunity window upon initial summoning.
  - Skeleton Horses:
    - Health: 53.0 HP (standard skeleton horse high health).
    - Movement speed: fast sprint (up to 12.0 m/s), jumping capability up to 3.5m.
    - Despawn timer: 15.0 minutes (18,000 ticks) if not engaged in combat by a player.
- **Mounted Combat Kinematics**:
  - Mounted archer AI: Circles player target at 8.0–16.0m standoff distance while firing arrows. Closes distance if > 16.0m, kites away if < 8.0m, and orbits tangentially within the standoff window.
  - Separate entity destruction: Killing horse dismounts rider to fight on foot; killing rider leaves tameable rideable skeleton horse for player!
  - Player riding: Freed skeleton horse can be mounted, ridden at 12.0 m/s with 3.5m jumping, and ridden underwater without drowning.
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/skeleton_trap_combat.zig` in `build.zig`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Implemented 30 comprehensive unit tests covering spawn probability scaling, thunderstorm requirements, proximity trigger radius, single-trigger mechanics, zero-damage cosmetic lightning, 4 horsemen transformation, spread spawn positions, rider equipment, horse stats, 60-tick summon invulnerability, iron helmet sunlight protection and durability breakage, 18,000-tick despawn timer countdown and combat halt, horse defeat dismount, rider defeat horse taming, player mounting and dismounting, underwater traversal, mounted archer standoff AI (closing in, retreating, circling), bow firing and enchanted damage, and encounter tracking.
