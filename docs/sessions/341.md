# Session 341: Milestone 223 — Detector Rails, Activator Rails & Cart Ejection System

## Summary
Implemented the full advanced rail transit mechanics: Detector Rails (`BlockId.detector_rail = 130`, `ItemType.detector_rail = 199`) with redstone power emission (`redstone.isSource`), Activator Rails (`BlockId.activator_rail = 131`, `ItemType.activator_rail = 200`), Recipe 142 and Recipe 143 crafting, and automatic passenger minecart ejection mechanics upon traversing activator rails.

## Changes
- **Blocks & World Properties (`src/world/block.zig`)**:
  - Added `BlockId.detector_rail = 130` and `BlockId.activator_rail = 131`.
  - Non-solid, transparent, hardness 0.7, stone footsteps.
  - Detector rail face texture `(4, 6)` (pressure plate contact tile); Activator rail face texture `(6, 7)` (redstone torch active crossties).
  - Emissive glow: detector rail 0.5, activator rail 0.55.
  - Included in `BlockId.is_rail()` predicate.
- **Redstone Logic (`src/world/redstone.zig`)**:
  - Detector rail marked as active redstone power source (`redstone.isSource(.detector_rail) == true`).
- **Items & Crafting Recipes (`src/inventory.zig`)**:
  - Added `ItemType.detector_rail = 199` and `ItemType.activator_rail = 200`.
  - Recipe 142: Detector Rail — 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Redstone Dust + 1 Stone/Pressure Plate -> 6 Detector Rails.
  - Recipe 143: Activator Rail — 2 Iron Ingots + 1 Redstone Torch + 1 Stick -> 6 Activator Rails.
- **Player Kinematics & Transit (`src/player.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - Added `Player.on_detector_rail: bool` and `Player.on_activator_rail: bool`.
  - Activator Rail passenger ejection: automatically dismounts player with upward hop (`vel_y = 5.0`), recovers minecart to inventory, and emits acoustic click + debris VFX.
