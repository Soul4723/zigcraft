# Session 325: Milestone 207 — Endermite Mob Variant, Pearl Infestation Spawning & Hostility Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the Endermite mob variant (`MobType.endermite`), tiny 4-segment crawling arthropod geometry with undulating crawl wave, Ender Pearl teleportation infestation spawning (15% probability), melee attack hostility, and ender pearl loot drops on defeat.

## Changes
- **Mob Variant & Drops (`src/inventory.zig`, `src/entity/mob.zig`)**:
  - Added `MobType.endermite` (8 HP, 0.4m width, 0.3m height, 2.8 m/s speed).
  - Sound mapping: `.mob_hiss`.
  - Mob defeat meat drop: drops `ItemType.ender_pearl`.
  - Unit tests: verified endermite stats, dimensions, sound mapping, and pearl drop.
- **Render Geometry (`src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Added 4-segment undulating crawling body geometry:
    - Head segment with glowing purple eyes texture `(7, 6)`.
    - Thorax segment with dark obsidian scales `(5, 2)`.
    - Abdomen segment.
    - Posterior tail segment.
    - Crawl animation: sinusoidal undulating horizontal wave (`anim_time * 12.0`).
- **Pearl Teleportation Infestation Spawning (`src/main.zig`)**:
  - On `t.kind == .ender_pearl` block impact: 15% probability spawns an Endermite at the player teleport arrival coordinate.
  - Plays `.mob_hiss` audio and logs spawn event.
  - Added `.endermite` to hostile mob melee attack loop dealing contact damage to player.
