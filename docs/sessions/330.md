# Session 330: Milestone 212 — Ocean Monument Generation, Guardian Mob Variant & Marine Combat Pipeline

## Summary
Implemented the submerged Ocean Monument procedural structure generation in the Overworld ocean, Guardian mob entity (`MobType.guardian`), 7-box cyclopean marine automaton geometry with kinetic spikes and tail fin, monument-anchored aquatic spawning, and guardian beam/thorns combat pipeline.

## Changes
- **Procedural Ocean Monument Structure (`src/world/terrain.zig`)**:
  - Added `getOceanMonumentBlock` in `TerrainGenerator`:
    - Footprint: wx in -8..8, wz in -24..-8, wy in 1..7 submerged below sea level.
    - Foundation: Dark Prismarine floor.
    - Exterior: alternating Prismarine and Prismarine Bricks perimeter walls with South entryway arch.
    - Pillars: 4 corner Dark Prismarine pillars capped with Sea Lantern beacons at (+-6, 5, -22 / -10).
    - Roof: stepped Prismarine Bricks and Sea Lantern pyramid crown.
    - Sanctuary: central altar chamber with Dark Prismarine pedestal, active Conduit core, and Sea Lantern cap at (0, 3, -16).
  - Integrated into `TerrainGenerator.generateChunk` for all ocean chunks covering the monument footprint.
- **Guardian Mob Entity (`src/inventory.zig`, `src/entity/mob.zig`, `src/entity/mob_renderer.zig`)**:
  - Added `MobType.guardian` (30 HP, 0.8m width, 0.8m height, 3.2 m/s swim speed in water, 0.5 m/s on land, sound `.mob_roar`).
  - Mob defeat meat drop: `.guardian => .prismarine_shard`.
  - 7-box marine automaton geometry:
    - 0.6m cuboid body with mottled prismarine scales.
    - Radiant cyclopean eye aperture on front face.
    - 4 kinetic defense spikes (dorsal, ventral, left, right) pulsing and retracting.
    - Articulated swimming tail fin undulating with propulsion wave.
- **Aquatic Spawner & Combat Loop (`src/entity/mob_manager.zig`, `src/main.zig`)**:
  - In `MobManager.ensureSpawnPopulation`: proximity to Ocean Monument coordinates preferentially summons Guardian sentinels.
  - Added `.guardian` to hostile attack loop dealing 4 damage with splash and roar audio feedback.
