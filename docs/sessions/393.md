# Session 393: Milestone 475 - Elytra Kinetic Glide & Firework Rocket Propulsion

## Goal
Implement Milestone 475: Elytra Kinetic Glide & Firework Rocket Propulsion in Zigcraft.

## What was done
- **Target Module**: Created `src/world/elytra_rocket_propulsion.zig`.
- **Elytra Gliding Flight Kinematics**:
  - `ElytraFlightState` state machine: `.grounded`, `.falling`, `.gliding`, `.kinetic_impact`.
  - Deployment activation condition: mid-air airborne state, equipped with Elytra (`is_equipped = true`), positive fall distance (`fall_distance > 0.0`), intact wings (`!is_broken` and `durability > 1`), and pressing jump spacebar.
  - Pitch angle aerodynamics (theta in `[-90, +90]` degrees):
    - Dive (theta < 0): converts potential energy into horizontal velocity: `v_horiz += -0.08 * @sin(theta_rad)`.
    - Climb (theta > 0): converts horizontal velocity into vertical altitude: `vy += 0.06 * @sin(theta_rad) - gravity`.
    - Drag decay: `0.99` per tick multiplier on horizontal and vertical velocity.
- **Firework Rocket Propulsion Boost**:
  - `boostWithRocket(duration_tier: u8)`:
    - Consumes 1 Firework Rocket item from inventory.
    - Adds forward acceleration vector along camera look direction: `look_vector * 1.5 m/s^2` per tick.
    - Duration scaling: Tier 1 (20 ticks / 1.0s), Tier 2 (40 ticks / 2.0s), Tier 3 (60 ticks / 3.0s).
    - Firework Star explosion hazard: rockets crafted with an explosive Firework Star detonate at the end of the boost dealing 5.0 HP direct blast damage to the player.
- **Kinetic Wall Crash Collision Damage**:
  - High-speed solid block face impact (`v >= 10.0 m/s`):
    - Inflicts kinetic damage: `damage = @max(0.0, (v - 10.0) * 2.0)`.
    - Resets forward velocity to 0 and cancels gliding flight (`.kinetic_impact` state).
  - Low-speed impacts (`< 10.0 m/s`): safe collision with zero damage.
- **Durability Consumption & Anvil Repair**:
  - Consumes 1 durability point per second of gliding flight (every 20 ticks).
  - At 1 durability point, wings break into Broken Elytra (`is_broken = true`), halting flight until repaired.
  - Anvil repair with Phantom Membranes restores 108 durability (25% of 432 max) per membrane, capped at 432, clearing broken status.
- **Build Registration**:
  - Registered `src/world/elytra_rocket_propulsion.zig` in `build.zig`.
- **Unit Tests**:
  - Implemented 35 unit tests covering all activation conditions, pitch dive/climb dynamics, drag decay, rocket boost duration tiers, inventory consumption, firework star blast hazard, kinetic crash damage threshold, low speed safety, durability wear, broken state, and Anvil repair.
