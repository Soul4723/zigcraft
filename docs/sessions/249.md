## Goal
Implement Respawn Anchor charging mechanics, respawn charge consumption, and dimensional overcharge detonation (Milestone 131).

  - Distinguishes charging with glowstone (0..4), spawn setting in the Nether, already-full rejection, uncharged warning, and Overworld overcharge detonation.

  - Right-click in the Overworld when charged triggers immediate 3-block radius crater explosion with player blast impulse and debris.
