# Session 347: Milestone 229 — Wooden and Iron Trapdoors, Physical Interaction & Redstone Actuation

## Summary
Implemented Wooden and Iron Trapdoors (`BlockId.wooden_trapdoor_closed`, `wooden_trapdoor_open`, `iron_trapdoor_closed`, `iron_trapdoor_open`) and their complete interaction / redstone / crafting pipelines:
- **Block & Item Definitions**:
  - `BlockId.wooden_trapdoor_closed = 140`, `wooden_trapdoor_open = 141` (hardness 0.5, flammable, wood footstep).
  - `BlockId.iron_trapdoor_closed = 142`, `iron_trapdoor_open = 143` (hardness 3.0, non-flammable, stone footstep).
  - Open trapdoors are non-solid (`is_solid() == false`); closed trapdoors are solid. Both are transparent (`is_transparent() == true`).
  - Face textures: wooden `(0, 6)` closed / `(1, 6)` open; iron `(2, 6)` closed / `(3, 6)` open.
  - `ItemType.wooden_trapdoor = 211`, `ItemType.iron_trapdoor = 212` (stack cap 64, roundtrip mappings to closed blocks).
- **Crafting**:
  - Recipe 154: 1 Wooden Door -> 2 Wooden Trapdoors.
  - Recipe 155: 1 Iron Door -> 2 Iron Trapdoors.
- **Physical & Redstone Interaction**:
  - `src/world/interact.zig`: added `toggleTrapdoor` and expanded `toggleWoodenDoor` to support wooden trapdoors with right-click / F-key manual toggle.
  - Manual interaction on iron trapdoor requires redstone power (consistent with iron doors).
  - `src/world/redstone.zig`: updated `targetDoorState` so redstone power (pressure plates, levers, torches, wires, detectors) opens closed trapdoors and deactivation closes open trapdoors.
